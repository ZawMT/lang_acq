# Basic Salutations and Everyday Phrases (인사말)

Korean greetings are set phrases, and many change with **politeness** (see [Speech levels](./07_speech_levels.md)) and with **who is leaving and who is staying**. Most are also said with a **bow** (a nod for casual situations, a deeper bow for formal ones).

## Hello

| Phrase | Level | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **안녕하십니까** | formal | presentations, service, very formal meetings |
| **안녕하세요** | polite | the everyday "hello" (any time of day) |
| **안녕** | casual | friends, children (both "hi" and "bye") |

The literal meaning is "Are you at peace?", but it's used like "hello", not as a real question. Answer with the same phrase.

Other common greetings:

- **식사하셨어요?** / **밥 먹었어?** (Have you eaten?) A friendly greeting, not an invitation. Answer simply: 네, 먹었어요.
- **오랜만이에요.** / **오랜만이야.** (Long time no see.)
- **잘 지냈어요?** / **잘 지냈어?** (Have you been well?) → **네, 잘 지냈어요.** (Yes, I've been well.)

## Goodbye

Korean has two different goodbyes, depending on **who leaves**:

| Phrase | Said to the person who... | Literally |
|---|---|---|
| **안녕히 가세요** | is **leaving** | go in peace |
| **안녕히 계세요** | is **staying** | stay in peace |
| **잘 가** | is leaving (casual) | go well |
| **잘 있어** | is staying (casual) | stay well |

- Leaving a shop: you say **안녕히 계세요**; the staff say **안녕히 가세요**.
- Both leaving: both say **안녕히 가세요**.

Other goodbyes:

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **들어가세요** | "Get home safely" (very common polite goodbye, esp. on the phone) |
| **또 봐요 / 또 봐** | See you again |
| **나중에 봐요 / 나중에 봐** | See you later |
| **내일 봐요** | See you tomorrow |
| **조심히 가세요** | Get home safely |

## First meeting

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **처음 뵙겠습니다** | How do you do? (formal; literally "I see you for the first time") |
| **만나서 반갑습니다 / 반가워요** | Nice to meet you |
| **저는 ___이에요 / 예요** | I'm ___ |
| **저는 ___(이)라고 합니다** | My name is ___ (formal) |
| **잘 부탁드립니다** | I look forward to working with you (literally "I ask for your good favor") |
| **성함이 어떻게 되세요?** | May I have your name? (polite) |
| **이름이 뭐예요?** | What's your name? |
| **어느 나라 사람이에요?** | What country are you from? |

- 안녕하세요. **처음 뵙겠습니다.** 저는 김민수**라고 합니다.** **잘 부탁드립니다.** (Hello. How do you do? My name is Kim Minsu. I look forward to working with you.)

## Thanks

| Phrase | Level |
|---|---|
| **감사합니다** | polite / formal (Sino-Korean) |
| **고맙습니다** | polite / formal (native) |
| **고마워요** | polite |
| **고마워** | casual |

Replies:

- **아니에요.** (Not at all.) (most common)
- **별말씀을요.** (Don't mention it.) (polite)
- **천만에요.** (You're welcome.) (textbook; rarely used in real conversation)

## Apologies

| Phrase | Level |
|---|---|
| **죄송합니다** | formal, deeper apology |
| **미안합니다 / 미안해요** | polite |
| **미안해** | casual |
| **실례합니다** | excuse me (before interrupting, passing through, entering) |

Reply: **괜찮아요.** (It's OK.)

## Getting attention and getting around

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **저기요** | Excuse me (to a stranger) |
| **여기요** | Excuse me! (calling restaurant staff) |
| **잠시만요 / 잠깐만요** | Just a moment; excuse me (let me through) |
| **실례지만...** | Excuse me, but... (before a question) |
| **___ 어디에 있어요?** | Where is ___? |
| **화장실 어디예요?** | Where's the restroom? |

## Meals

| Phrase | When | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **잘 먹겠습니다** | before eating (esp. when treated) | I'll eat well / thank you for the food |
| **잘 먹었습니다** | after eating | I ate well / thank you for the meal |
| **맛있게 드세요** | to someone about to eat | Enjoy your meal |
| **많이 드세요** | host to guest | Please eat a lot |

## Work and effort

| Phrase | When | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **수고하셨습니다** | after work, to colleagues | Thanks for your hard work |
| **수고하세요** | leaving a shop, to staff | Keep up the good work |
| **고생하셨습니다** | after something tough | You've been through a lot |
| **먼저 가 보겠습니다** | leaving the office before others | I'll be going first |
| **힘내세요 / 화이팅!** | encouragement | Cheer up / You can do it! |

**Trap:** **수고하세요** and **수고하셨습니다** are said to equals or people below you. Saying them to someone clearly senior can sound presumptuous; use **감사합니다** instead.

## Home

| Phrase | Said by | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **다녀오겠습니다** | the person leaving home | I'll be back (literally "I'll go and come back") |
| **다녀오세요 / 잘 다녀와** | the person staying | See you later / Have a good day |
| **다녀왔습니다** | the person returning | I'm home |
| **잘 다녀왔어요?** | the person at home | Welcome back |
| **안녕히 주무세요 / 잘 자** | at bedtime | Good night |
| **안녕히 주무셨어요? / 잘 잤어?** | in the morning | Did you sleep well? |

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **여보세요** | Hello (on the phone) |

| **다시 전화할게요** | I'll call you back |
| **들어가세요** | (polite goodbye before hanging up) |

## When you don't understand

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **다시 한번 말씀해 주시겠어요?** | Could you say that again? |
| **천천히 말해 주세요** | Please speak slowly |
| **잘 모르겠어요** | I'm not sure / I don't understand |
| **한국어를 잘 못해요** | I don't speak Korean well |
| **영어 할 줄 아세요?** | Do you speak English? |
| **___ 이/가 무슨 뜻이에요?** | What does ___ mean? |
| **한국어로 뭐라고 해요?** | How do you say it in Korean? |
| **알겠습니다 / 알겠어요** | Understood / Got it |
