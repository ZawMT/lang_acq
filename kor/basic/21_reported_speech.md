# Reported Speech (인용)

Korean reports what someone said with a **quotation ending** + **하다** (or another speech verb). There are two kinds:

| Kind | Marker | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **직접 인용** (direct) | "..." **(이)라고** 하다 | 민수가 "배고파요."**라고** 했어요. (Minsu said, "I'm hungry.") |
| **간접 인용** (indirect) | plain form + **고** 하다 | 민수가 배고프**다고** 했어요. (Minsu said he was hungry.) |

## Direct quotation

Put the exact words in quotation marks, followed by **라고** (or **하고** when imitating sounds or tone exactly):

- 선생님이 "숙제 내세요."**라고** 말씀하셨어요. (The teacher said, "Hand in your homework.")
- 아이가 "엄마!"**하고** 불렀어요. (The child called out, "Mom!")

Direct quotation is mostly used in writing. In conversation, indirect is far more common.

## Indirect quotation

Indirect quotation is built on the **plain style (해라체)** of the original sentence, plus **-고**. The ending depends on the **sentence type** of what was said.

### Statements: -다고

| Predicate | Form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| verb (present) | **-ㄴ다고 / -는다고** | 간**다고**, 먹**는다고** |
| adjective (present) | **-다고** | 좋**다고**, 바쁘**다고** |
| past | **-았/었다고** | 갔**다고**, 좋았**다고** |
| future | **-(으)ㄹ 거라고** / **-겠다고** | 갈 **거라고**, 가**겠다고** |
| noun + 이다 | **-(이)라고** | 학생**이라고**, 의사**라고** |
| 아니다 | **아니라고** | 학생이 **아니라고** |

- 민수 씨가 내일 **온다고** 했어요. (Minsu said he'd come tomorrow.)
- 친구가 그 영화가 재미**있다고** 했어요. (My friend said that movie was fun.)
- 지민 씨는 자기가 **학생이라고** 했어요. (Jimin said she was a student.)

### Questions: -냐고

| Predicate | Form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| verb, 있다/없다 | **-(느)냐고** | 가**냐고** / 가**느냐고** |
| adjective | **-(으)냐고** | 좋**으냐고** / 좋**냐고** |
| noun + 이다 | **-(이)냐고** | 학생**이냐고** |

In speech, **-냐고** is used for everything: 가냐고, 먹냐고, 좋냐고.

- 선생님이 숙제를 했**냐고** 물어보셨어요. (The teacher asked if I'd done my homework.)
- 친구가 어디 **가냐고** 물었어요. (My friend asked where I was going.)

The usual verb for reporting questions is **묻다 / 물어보다** (to ask).

### Commands: -(으)라고

| Form | Example |
|---|---|
| **-(으)라고** | 가**라고**, 먹**으라고** |
| **-지 말라고** (negative) | 가**지 말라고** |

- 의사가 푹 쉬**라고** 했어요. (The doctor told me to get plenty of rest.)
- 엄마가 늦게 다니**지 말라고** 했어요. (Mom told me not to stay out late.)

### Suggestions: -자고

- 친구가 같이 영화 보**자고** 했어요. (My friend suggested watching a movie together.)
- 오늘은 나가**지 말자고** 했어요. (She suggested we not go out today.)

## Requests: 달라고 vs 주라고

"Give" in a reported command splits in two, depending on **who receives**:

| Receiver | Form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| the **original speaker** | **-아/어 달라고** | 민수가 (자기를) 도와 **달라고** 했어요. (Minsu asked [me] to help him.) |
| **someone else** | **-아/어 주라고** | 민수가 동생을 도와 **주라고** 했어요. (Minsu told me to help my younger sibling.) |

- 물 좀 **달라고** 했어요. (She asked for some water.)
- 이 책을 지민 씨한테 **주라고** 했어요. (He told me to give this book to Jimin.)

## Contracted forms (spoken)

In conversation, **-고 해요** usually contracts. These forms are extremely common:

| Full | Contracted | Example |
|---|---|---|
| -다고 해요 | **-대요** | 내일 비가 온**대요**. (They say it'll rain tomorrow.) |
| -(이)라고 해요 | **-(이)래요** | 저 사람이 선생님**이래요**. (They say that person is a teacher.) |
| -냐고 해요 | **-냬요** | 언제 오**냬요**. (She's asking when you're coming.) |
| -(으)라고 해요 | **-(으)래요** | 빨리 오**래요**. (He says to come quickly.) |
| -자고 해요 | **-재요** | 같이 가**재요**. (She says let's go together.) |
| -다고 했어요 | **-댔어요** | 괜찮**댔어요**. (He said it was fine.) |

**-대요** is also the everyday way to say "I heard that..." or "apparently":

- 그 식당 맛있**대요**. (I hear that restaurant is good.)

**Trap:** **-대요** (reported) and **-데요** (the speaker's own past observation, from -더-) look and sound alike: 맛있**대요** (they say it's tasty) vs 맛있**데요** (I found it tasty).

## Other quoting verbs

**하다** is the default, but other verbs can replace it:

| Verb | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **말하다** | say | 괜찮다고 **말했어요**. (He said it was fine.) |
| **묻다 / 물어보다** | ask | 몇 살이냐고 **물어봤어요**. (She asked how old I was.) |
| **듣다** | hear | 결혼한다고 **들었어요**. (I heard you're getting married.) |
| **생각하다** | think | 맞다고 **생각해요**. (I think it's right.) |
| **부탁하다** | ask a favor | 도와 달라고 **부탁했어요**. (He asked me to help.) |

## Changes when reporting

As in any reported speech, pronouns and time words shift to the reporter's point of view:

- 민수: "**저는** **내일** 가요." (I'm going tomorrow.)
- Reported the next day: 민수가 **자기는** **오늘** 간다고 했어요. (Minsu said he's going today.)

Use **자기** for the original speaker referring to themselves (see [Pronouns](./08_pronouns.md)). Honorifics follow **your** relationship to the people involved, not the original speaker's.

## Emphasis: -다고요, -다니까요

Adding -요 directly to a quotation form repeats or insists:

- 제가 **했다고요**. (I *said* I did it.)
- 괜찮**다니까요**. (I'm telling you, it's fine.)
- 뭐**라고요**? (What did you say?)
