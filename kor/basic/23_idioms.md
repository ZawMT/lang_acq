# Idioms and Fixed Expressions (관용 표현)

Korean has three big families of fixed expressions:

| Type | What it is | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **관용구** (idiom) | a phrase whose meaning isn't the sum of its words | 발이 넓다 (to know many people) |
| **속담** (proverb) | a traditional saying with a lesson | 누워서 떡 먹기 (a piece of cake) |
| **사자성어** (four-character idiom) | a Sino-Korean expression of four hanja | 일석이조 (two birds with one stone) |

Plus **collocations**: fixed verb + noun pairings you just have to learn.

## Body-part idioms (관용구)

A huge number of Korean idioms are built on body parts.

### 눈 (eye)

| Idiom | Literally | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **눈이 높다** | eyes are high | to have high standards, be picky |
| **눈치가 빠르다** | quick at 눈치 | to read the room well |
| **눈치가 없다** | no 눈치 | to be oblivious to the mood |
| **눈 깜짝할 사이** | the time it takes to blink | in the blink of an eye |
| **눈에 넣어도 아프지 않다** | wouldn't hurt even in the eye | to adore (a child) |

- 제 친구는 **눈이 높아서** 아직 여자 친구가 없어요. (My friend has high standards, so he still doesn't have a girlfriend.)

**눈치** (literally "eye-measure") is a key cultural concept: sensing unspoken feelings and the atmosphere, and acting accordingly. **눈치를 보다** = to watch others' reactions carefully, to tread carefully.

### 입 (mouth)

| Idiom | Literally | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **입이 무겁다** | mouth is heavy | to keep secrets well |
| **입이 가볍다** | mouth is light | to be a blabbermouth |
| **입이 짧다** | mouth is short | to be a picky eater |
| **입에 맞다** | fits the mouth | to suit one's taste |
| **입을 모으다** | gather mouths | to say with one voice |

- 음식이 **입에 맞으세요**? (Is the food to your liking?)

### 손 (hand)

| Idiom | Literally | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **손이 크다** | hands are big | to be generous (esp. with food portions) |
| **손이 많이 가다** | many hands go | to take a lot of work |
| **손을 떼다** | take one's hand off | to quit, wash one's hands of |
| **손발이 맞다** | hands and feet match | to work well together |

- 할머니는 **손이 커서** 음식을 항상 많이 하세요. (Grandma is generous, so she always makes lots of food.)

### 발, 귀, 머리, 배, 간, 얼굴

| Idiom | Literally | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **발이 넓다** | feet are wide | to know many people |
| **발을 끊다** | cut off the feet | to stop visiting (a place) |
| **귀가 얇다** | ears are thin | to be easily persuaded, gullible |
| **귀에 못이 박히다** | a nail is driven into the ear | to be sick of hearing it |
| **머리를 식히다** | cool the head | to clear one's head, take a break |
| **머리가 좋다** | head is good | to be smart |
| **배가 아프다** | stomach hurts | to be jealous (of someone's success) |
| **간이 크다** | liver is big | to be bold, daring |
| **얼굴이 두껍다** | face is thick | to be shameless |
| **얼굴이 넓다** | face is wide | to know many people (= 발이 넓다) |

- 민수 씨는 **발이 넓어서** 모르는 사람이 없어요. (Minsu knows everybody.)

## Other everyday idioms

| Idiom | Literally | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **미역국을 먹다** | eat seaweed soup | to fail (an exam) (slippery seaweed = "slip") |
| **국수를 먹다** | eat noodles | to get married (noodles are served at weddings) |
| **바가지를 쓰다** | wear a gourd | to be ripped off, overcharged |
| **한턱내다** | treat one jaw | to treat someone (pay for food) |
| **파김치가 되다** | become green-onion kimchi | to be exhausted, limp |
| **식은 죽 먹기** | eating cold porridge | very easy |
| **그림의 떡** | rice cake in a picture | something you can't have |

- 이번 시험에서 **미역국을 먹었어요**. (I failed this exam.)
- 오늘은 제가 **한턱낼게요**. (Today's on me.)

## Proverbs (속담)

| Proverb | Literally | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **누워서 떡 먹기** | eating rice cake lying down | a piece of cake |
| **가는 말이 고와야 오는 말이 곱다** | words going out must be kind for words coming back to be kind | treat others as you want to be treated |
| **낮말은 새가 듣고 밤말은 쥐가 듣는다** | birds hear day words, mice hear night words | the walls have ears |
| **세 살 버릇 여든까지 간다** | a habit at three lasts until eighty | old habits die hard |
| **호랑이도 제 말 하면 온다** | even the tiger comes when you talk of it | speak of the devil |
| **원숭이도 나무에서 떨어진다** | even monkeys fall from trees | everyone makes mistakes |
| **소 잃고 외양간 고친다** | fix the barn after losing the cow | too little, too late |
| **금강산도 식후경** | even Mt. Geumgang comes after eating | food first (nothing's fun on an empty stomach) |
| **고생 끝에 낙이 온다** | joy comes at the end of hardship | no pain, no gain |
| **백지장도 맞들면 낫다** | even a sheet of paper is easier lifted together | many hands make light work |
| **등잔 밑이 어둡다** | it's dark under the lamp | hard to see what's right under your nose |
| **발 없는 말이 천 리 간다** | a word without feet travels a thousand 리 | rumors spread fast |
| **티끌 모아 태산** | gather dust to make a great mountain | every little bit counts |

## Four-character idioms (사자성어)

Sino-Korean expressions of four hanja, common in writing, speeches, and news (see [Hangul: Chinese characters](./01_hangul.md)).

| Idiom | Hanja | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **일석이조** | 一石二鳥 | two birds with one stone |
| **작심삼일** | 作心三日 | resolutions that last three days |
| **자업자득** | 自業自得 | you reap what you sow |
| **유유상종** | 類類相從 | birds of a feather flock together |
| **이심전심** | 以心傳心 | understanding each other without words |
| **동문서답** | 東問西答 | an irrelevant answer (ask east, answer west) |
| **우왕좌왕** | 右往左往 | going this way and that, in confusion |
| **대기만성** | 大器晩成 | great talents mature late |
| **십중팔구** | 十中八九 | nine times out of ten, most likely |
| **천생연분** | 天生緣分 | a match made in heaven |
| **금시초문** | 今始初聞 | the first I've heard of it |

- 새해 계획이 또 **작심삼일**이 됐어요. (My New Year's resolution fizzled out again.)
- 그건 **금시초문**인데요. (That's the first I've heard of it.)

## Collocations (연어)

Fixed verb pairings. Many use a verb related to the noun itself:

| Collocation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **꿈을 꾸다** | to dream (a dream) |
| **잠을 자다** | to sleep (a sleep) |
| **춤을 추다** | to dance (a dance) |
| **사진을 찍다** | to take a photo |
| **전화를 걸다 / 받다 / 끊다** | to make / answer / hang up a call |
| **감기에 걸리다** | to catch a cold |
| **시간이 걸리다** | to take time |
| **마음에 들다** | to like (literally "enter the heart") |
| **신경(을) 쓰다** | to pay attention, worry about |
| **나이를 먹다** | to get older (literally "eat age") |
| **약속을 지키다 / 어기다** | to keep / break a promise |
| **결정을 내리다** | to make a decision |
| **샤워를 하다** | to take a shower |
| **머리를 감다** | to wash one's hair |
| **이를 닦다** | to brush one's teeth |
| **돈을 벌다 / 쓰다 / 모으다** | to earn / spend / save money |

- 이 옷이 **마음에 들어요**. (I like these clothes.)
- 신경 **쓰지 마세요**. (Don't worry about it.)
