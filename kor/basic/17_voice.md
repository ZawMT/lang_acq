# Voice: Passive and Causative (피동과 사동)

**Voice** changes who the sentence is about:

| Voice | Focus | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **능동** (active) | the doer | 경찰이 도둑을 **잡았어요**. (The police caught the thief.) |
| **피동** (passive) | the one affected | 도둑이 경찰에게 **잡혔어요**. (The thief was caught by the police.) |
| **사동** (causative) | someone who makes/lets another act | 엄마가 아이에게 밥을 **먹였어요**. (The mother fed the child.) |

The passive is used sparingly in Korean. Speakers usually prefer an active sentence with the subject dropped, or an intransitive verb (see [Verbs overview](./13_verbs_overview.md)).

## Passive (피동)

There are four ways to make a passive:

| Method | Added to | Example |
|---|---|---|
| suffix **-이/히/리/기-** | certain native verbs | 잡다 → 잡**히**다 (be caught) |
| **-아/어지다** | most other verbs | 만들다 → 만들**어지다** (be made) |
| **되다** | 하다 verbs | 사용하다 → 사용**되다** (be used) |
| **받다 / 당하다** | some 하다 nouns | 사랑**받다** (be loved) |

### Suffix passives: -이/히/리/기-

These are separate dictionary words, and which suffix a verb takes must be learned:

| Suffix | Active → Passive |
|---|---|
| **-이-** | 보다 → **보이다** (be seen, be visible), 쓰다 → **쓰이다** (be written, be used), 놓다 → **놓이다** (be placed), 바꾸다 → **바뀌다** (be changed) |
| **-히-** | 잡다 → **잡히다** (be caught), 먹다 → **먹히다** (be eaten), 닫다 → **닫히다** (be closed), 읽다 → **읽히다** (be read), 막다 → **막히다** (be blocked) |
| **-리-** | 열다 → **열리다** (be opened), 팔다 → **팔리다** (be sold), 듣다 → **들리다** (be heard), 걸다 → **걸리다** (be hung; be caught), 풀다 → **풀리다** (be solved, be untied) |
| **-기-** | 안다 → **안기다** (be hugged), 쫓다 → **쫓기다** (be chased), 끊다 → **끊기다** (be cut off), 씻다 → **씻기다** (be washed) |

- 창문이 **열렸어요**. (The window opened / was opened.)
- 이 책은 많이 **팔렸어요**. (This book sold well.)
- 밖에서 이상한 소리가 **들려요**. (I hear a strange sound outside.)
- 길이 많이 **막혀요**. (The roads are really jammed.)

The object of the active sentence becomes the subject (**이/가**). The doer, if mentioned, takes **에게 / 한테** (people, animals) or **에** (things):

- 모기**한테** 물렸어요. (I got bitten by a mosquito.)
- 옷이 못**에** 걸렸어요. (My clothes got caught on a nail.)

Many of these passives are everyday words in their own right, not "passive" in feel: **보이다** (to be visible, to show), **들리다** (to be audible), **막히다** (to be jammed), **걸리다** (to take [time]; to catch [a cold]):

- 시간이 오래 **걸려요**. (It takes a long time.)
- 감기에 **걸렸어요**. (I caught a cold.)

### -아/어지다

Works with most verbs that don't have a suffix passive. It often suggests the result came about **naturally or without effort**:

- 이 글씨가 잘 안 **지워져요**. (This writing won't erase.)
- 이 건물은 1990년에 **만들어졌어요**. (This building was built in 1990.)
- 불이 **켜졌어요**. (The light came on.)

With **adjectives**, -아/어지다 means "become" (not passive): 추**워졌어요** (It got cold). See [Adjectives](./11_adjectives.md).

**Trap:** don't stack two passives. ✗ 쓰여지다 (쓰이- + -어지다) is common but non-standard; the standard forms are **쓰이다** or **써지다**.

### 되다 for 하다 verbs

Replace **하다** with **되다**:

| Active | Passive |
|---|---|
| 사용하다 (use) | **사용되다** (be used) |
| 취소하다 (cancel) | **취소되다** (be cancelled) |
| 시작하다 (start) | **시작되다** (begin) |
| 결정하다 (decide) | **결정되다** (be decided) |
| 연결하다 (connect) | **연결되다** (be connected) |

- 비행기가 **취소됐어요**. (The flight was cancelled.)
- 영화가 곧 **시작돼요**. (The movie is starting soon.)

### 받다 and 당하다

With some nouns, **받다** (receive) makes a positive or neutral passive, **당하다** (suffer) a negative one:

| 받다 | 당하다 |
|---|---|
| 사랑**받다** (be loved) | 무시**당하다** (be ignored) |
| 초대**받다** (be invited) | 사기**당하다** (be scammed) |
| 칭찬**받다** (be praised) | 거절**당하다** (be rejected) |
| 선택**받다** (be chosen) | 이용**당하다** (be used, exploited) |

## Causative (사동)

A causative says someone **makes or lets** another person (or thing) do something.

### Suffix causatives: -이/히/리/기/우/구/추-

Like suffix passives, these are separate words and must be learned. Some verbs take the **same suffix** for both passive and causative.

| Suffix | Base → Causative |
|---|---|
| **-이-** | 먹다 → **먹이다** (feed), 죽다 → **죽이다** (kill), 보다 → **보이다** (show), 끓다 → **끓이다** (boil) |
| **-히-** | 앉다 → **앉히다** (seat someone), 입다 → **입히다** (dress someone), 읽다 → **읽히다** (make someone read), 눕다 → **눕히다** (lay down) |
| **-리-** | 울다 → **울리다** (make cry; ring), 살다 → **살리다** (save a life), 알다 → **알리다** (inform), 날다 → **날리다** (fly [a kite]) |
| **-기-** | 웃다 → **웃기다** (make laugh; be funny), 벗다 → **벗기다** (undress someone), 신다 → **신기다** (put shoes on someone), 맡다 → **맡기다** (entrust) |
| **-우-** | 자다 → **재우다** (put to sleep), 타다 → **태우다** (give a ride; burn), 깨다 → **깨우다** (wake someone), 비다 → **비우다** (empty) |
| **-구-** | 달다 → **달구다** (heat up) |
| **-추-** | 맞다 → **맞추다** (fit, adjust), 낮다 → **낮추다** (lower), 늦다 → **늦추다** (delay) |

- 엄마가 아기를 **재웠어요**. (The mother put the baby to sleep.)
- 이 사진 좀 **보여** 주세요. (Please show me this photo.)
- 그 영화 진짜 **웃겨요**. (That movie is really funny.)
- 역까지 **태워** 줄게요. (I'll give you a ride to the station.)

**Trap:** **보이다** and **읽히다** can be passive or causative. Particles tell you which:

- 산이 **보여요**. (The mountain is visible.) (passive)
- 친구에게 사진을 **보여** 줬어요. (I showed my friend a photo.) (causative)

### -게 하다

Works with almost any verb or adjective. It's more **indirect** than the suffix form: you cause the situation, and the other person does the action themselves.

- 선생님이 학생들을 일찍 집에 **가게 했어요**. (The teacher let the students go home early.)
- 엄마가 아이에게 밥을 **먹게 했어요**. (The mother had the child eat.)
- 걱정**하게 해서** 미안해요. (Sorry for making you worry.)

Compare:

| Suffix (direct) | -게 하다 (indirect) |
|---|---|
| 아이에게 밥을 **먹였어요** (fed the child, e.g. with a spoon) | 아이에게 밥을 **먹게 했어요** (made/let the child eat) |

### 시키다

For **하다** verbs, replace 하다 with **시키다** ("make someone do"):

- 공부하다 → 아이에게 공부를 **시켰어요**. (I made the child study.)
- 청소하다 → 동생한테 청소를 **시켰어요**. (I made my sibling clean.)

**시키다** also means "to order (food)": 짜장면 두 그릇 **시켰어요**. (I ordered two bowls of jjajangmyeon.)
