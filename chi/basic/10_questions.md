# Questions (疑問句)

Chinese questions **keep statement word order**. There are four main ways to ask.

## 1. Statement + 嗎

Add **嗎** ˙ㄇㄚ *ma* to the end of a statement for a yes/no question:

> 你是學生。 → 你是學生**嗎**？ *Nǐ shì xuéshēng ma?* (Are you a student?)
> 你喜歡咖啡**嗎**？ → (Do you like coffee?)

## 2. A-not-A

Say the verb or adjective, then 不 + the same word:

> 你**去不去**？ → *Nǐ qù bu qù?* (Are you going?)
> 這個**好不好**吃？ → (Is this tasty?)
> 你**有沒有**時間？ → *Nǐ yǒu méiyǒu shíjiān?* (Do you have time?)
> 他**是不是**老師？ → (Is he a teacher?)

- With two-syllable words, the first can be shortened: **喜不喜歡** = 喜歡不喜歡.
- 不 in the middle is usually **neutral tone**: *qù bu qù*.
- **No 嗎** with A-not-A: 你去不去嗎 ✗.
- **No 很, 也, 都** before A-not-A: 很好不好 ✗.
- For past events, use **…了沒有** or **有沒有…**: 你吃了沒有？ / 你有沒有吃？ (Did you eat?)

嗎 is neutral; A-not-A can sound a bit more direct or insistent.

## 3. Question words stay in place

Put the question word **where the answer will go**. No change in order, and **no 嗎**:

> 他是**誰**？ → 他是**王老師**。 (Who is he? — He's Mr Wang.)
> 你叫**什麼**名字？ → 我叫**小明**。 (What's your name? — I'm Xiaoming.)
> 你去**哪裡**？ → 我去**學校**。 (Where are you going? — To school.)
> 你**什麼時候**來？ → 我**明天**來。 (When are you coming? — Tomorrow.)

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 誰 | who |
| 什麼 | what |
| 哪 + measure | which |
| 哪裡 / 哪兒 | where |
| 什麼時候 | when |
| 怎麼 | how |
| 為什麼 | why |
| 幾 / 多少 | how many |
| 怎麼樣 | how is it? |

### 怎麼 vs 為什麼

| | Feel | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **為什麼** | neutral "why" | 你**為什麼**學中文？ (Why are you studying Chinese?) |
| **怎麼** + verb | "how" | 這個字**怎麼**念？ (How do you read this character?) |
| **怎麼** + negative / surprise | "how come" | 你**怎麼**沒來？ (How come you didn't come?) |

### 幾 vs 多少

| | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **幾** | expected small number; needs a measure word | 你有**幾**個孩子？ (How many children do you have?) |
| **多少** | any number; measure word optional | 你們學校有**多少**學生？ (How many students does your school have?) |

幾 is also used for dates and time: **幾**點？ (What time?), **幾**號？ (What date?).

## 4. Choice: 還是

**還是** ㄏㄞˊ ㄕˋ *háishì* = "or" in questions:

> 你要茶**還是**咖啡？ → *Nǐ yào chá háishì kāfēi?* (Tea or coffee?)

In statements, "or" is **或者** / **或是**: 茶或者咖啡都可以。 (Tea or coffee are both fine.)

## Other question particles

| Particle | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **呢** ˙ㄋㄜ *ne* | "and you?", "what about…?" | 我很好，你**呢**？ (I'm fine, and you?) |
| | "where is…?" | 我的手機**呢**？ (Where's my phone?) |
| | softens a question-word question | 你在想什麼**呢**？ (What are you thinking about?) |
| **吧** ˙ㄅㄚ *ba* | "I suppose…, right?" | 你是學生**吧**？ (You're a student, right?) |

## Tag questions

Add a short question at the end to check:

| Tag | Example |
|---|---|
| 好嗎？ / 好不好？ | 我們走吧，**好嗎**？ (Let's go, OK?) |
| 對不對？ / 對吧？ | 他是台灣人，**對不對**？ (He's Taiwanese, right?) |
| 是不是？ | 你累了，**是不是**？ (You're tired, aren't you?) |
| 可以嗎？ | 我坐這裡，**可以嗎**？ (May I sit here?) |

## Answering yes and no

Chinese has **no single word** for "yes" or "no". Answer by **repeating the verb** (or adjective):

> 你去嗎？ → **去**。/ **不去**。 (Are you going? — Yes. / No.)
> 你有時間嗎？ → **有**。/ **沒有**。 (Do you have time? — Yes. / No.)
> 好吃嗎？ → **好吃**。/ **不好吃**。 (Is it good? — Yes. / No.)

**是** / **不是** and **對** / **不對** work for questions with 是 or for confirming facts:

> 你是學生嗎？ → **是**。 (Yes.)
> 他是台灣人，對嗎？ → **對**。 (Right.)

**Answering negative questions:** Chinese answers the **statement**, not the fact. "Yes" means "what you said is right":

> 你不去嗎？ → **對**，我不去。 (Aren't you going? — **Right**, I'm not going.)
