# Zhuyin (注音符號)

**注音符號** (*zhùyīn fúhào*, "sound-marking symbols") is a set of **phonetic symbols** for writing Mandarin pronunciation. It is usually called **Zhuyin**, or informally **Bopomofo**, after its first four symbols: **ㄅㄆㄇㄈ** (*bo po mo fo*).

Zhuyin is **not** a way of writing Chinese. Chinese is written in characters (漢字). Zhuyin shows **how a character is said**, the same job Pinyin does with Latin letters:

| Character | Zhuyin | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 你好 | ㄋㄧˇ ㄏㄠˇ | nǐ hǎo | hello |
| 台灣 | ㄊㄞˊ ㄨㄢ | Táiwān | Taiwan |
| 中文 | ㄓㄨㄥ ㄨㄣˊ | Zhōngwén | Chinese language |

It was created in **1913** and made official in **1918** by the Republic of China. Today it is used mainly in **Taiwan**:

- Children learn Zhuyin in the first weeks of primary school, before characters.
- Children's books and textbooks print it beside the characters (in vertical text, to the right of each character).
- Dictionaries in Taiwan give pronunciations in Zhuyin.
- Many people in Taiwan type Chinese with a Zhuyin keyboard (see [Typing](#typing-with-zhuyin)).

The shapes come from **old characters or parts of characters** that had a similar sound:

| Symbol | From | Symbol | From |
|---|---|---|---|
| ㄅ b | 勹 | ㄕ sh | 尸 |
| ㄇ m | 冂 | ㄖ r | 日 |
| ㄈ f | 匚 | ㄙ s | 厶 |
| ㄉ d | 刀 | ㄚ a | 丫 |
| ㄋ n | 乃 | ㄧ i | 一 |
| ㄌ l | 力 | ㄠ ao | 幺 |
| ㄔ ch | 彳 | ㄦ er | 儿 |

## The 37 symbols

Zhuyin has **21 initials** (consonants at the start of a syllable), **3 medials** (glide vowels in the middle), and **13 finals** (the vowel or vowel + ending).

### Initials (聲母)

Each initial is named with a vowel attached so it can be said aloud. Learners recite them in groups, in this order:

| Group | Symbols | Pinyin | Said as |
|---|---|---|---|
| lips | ㄅ ㄆ ㄇ ㄈ | b p m f | bo po mo fo |
| tongue tip | ㄉ ㄊ ㄋ ㄌ | d t n l | de te ne le |
| back of tongue | ㄍ ㄎ ㄏ | g k h | ge ke he |
| tongue flat, front | ㄐ ㄑ ㄒ | j q x | ji qi xi |
| tongue curled back | ㄓ ㄔ ㄕ ㄖ | zh ch sh r | zhi chi shi ri |
| tongue tip, teeth | ㄗ ㄘ ㄙ | z c s | zi ci si |

**Aspiration:** in each pair below, the second sound has a strong puff of air. That puff, not voicing, is what tells them apart:

| No puff | With puff |
|---|---|
| ㄅ b | ㄆ p |
| ㄉ d | ㄊ t |
| ㄍ g | ㄎ k |
| ㄐ j | ㄑ q |
| ㄓ zh | ㄔ ch |
| ㄗ z | ㄘ c |

### Medials (介音)

| Symbol | Pinyin | Alone (Pinyin) |
|---|---|---|
| ㄧ | i | yi |
| ㄨ | u | wu |
| ㄩ | ü | yu |

A medial can be a syllable on its own (ㄧ *yī* (one), ㄨˇ *wǔ* (five), ㄩˊ *yú* (fish)), or sit between an initial and a final (ㄐ**ㄧ**ㄠ *jiao*, ㄏ**ㄨ**ㄚ *hua*).

**Trap:** ㄧ is written **vertically (丨) in horizontal text** and horizontally (一) in vertical text, so it doesn't look like a dash or the character 一.

### Finals (韻母)

| Type | Symbols | Pinyin |
|---|---|---|
| single vowels | ㄚ ㄛ ㄜ ㄝ | a o e ê |
| vowel pairs | ㄞ ㄟ ㄠ ㄡ | ai ei ao ou |
| with -n | ㄢ ㄣ | an en |
| with -ng | ㄤ ㄥ | ang eng |
| retroflex | ㄦ | er |

ㄝ almost never appears alone. It is used after a medial: ㄧㄝ *ie*, ㄩㄝ *üe*.

## Building a syllable

A syllable has **at most three symbols**, always in this order, plus a tone mark:

> **initial** + **medial** + **final** + tone

| Built from | Syllable | Pinyin | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| final only | ㄞˋ | ài | 愛 (love) |
| initial + final | ㄇㄚ | mā | 媽 (mother) |
| medial only | ㄨˇ | wǔ | 五 (five) |
| initial + medial | ㄌㄩˋ | lǜ | 綠 (green) |
| medial + final | ㄧㄠˋ | yào | 要 (want) |
| initial + medial + final | ㄓㄨㄤ | zhuāng | 裝 (to install) |
| initial + medial + final | ㄒㄩㄝˊ | xué | 學 (to study) |
| initial only | ㄕ | shī | 詩 (poem) |

## Tones (聲調)

| Tone | Mark | Example | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1st (high, level) | none | ㄇㄚ | mā | 媽 mother |
| 2nd (rising) | ˊ | ㄇㄚˊ | má | 麻 hemp |
| 3rd (low, dipping) | ˇ | ㄇㄚˇ | mǎ | 馬 horse |
| 4th (falling) | ˋ | ㄇㄚˋ | mà | 罵 to scold |
| neutral (light, short) | ˙ | ˙ㄇㄚ | ma | 嗎 question particle |

- The **1st tone has no mark**. (A bar ˉ exists, but it is almost never written.)
- Tone marks go **after** the syllable in horizontal text, and **to the right** of it in vertical text.
- The **neutral tone mark ˙ goes before** the syllable (above it in vertical text): 謝謝 ㄒㄧㄝˋ ˙ㄒㄧㄝ, 媽媽 ㄇㄚ ˙ㄇㄚ.

## Zhuyin and Pinyin spelling

The sounds are the same, but Pinyin uses some spelling rules that Zhuyin doesn't need. Zhuyin writes each sound **as it is**, so these are the places to watch when switching between the two.

### 1. zhi chi shi ri zi ci si: initial alone

Pinyin adds an **-i** that is not a real "i" sound. Zhuyin writes only the initial:

| Pinyin | Zhuyin | Example |
|---|---|---|
| zhi | ㄓ | 知 ㄓ (to know) |
| chi | ㄔ | 吃 ㄔ (to eat) |
| shi | ㄕ | 是 ㄕˋ (to be) |
| ri | ㄖ | 日 ㄖˋ (day, sun) |
| zi | ㄗ | 字 ㄗˋ (character) |
| ci | ㄘ | 次 ㄘˋ (time, occurrence) |
| si | ㄙ | 四 ㄙˋ (four) |

### 2. Pinyin y and w are just ㄧ ㄨ ㄩ

Pinyin writes **y** and **w** at the start of a syllable that begins with a medial. Zhuyin has no such letters:

| Pinyin | Zhuyin | Pinyin | Zhuyin |
|---|---|---|---|
| yi | ㄧ | wu | ㄨ |
| ya | ㄧㄚ | wa | ㄨㄚ |
| ye | ㄧㄝ | wo | ㄨㄛ |
| yao | ㄧㄠ | wai | ㄨㄞ |
| you | ㄧㄡ | wei | ㄨㄟ |
| yan | ㄧㄢ | wan | ㄨㄢ |
| yin | ㄧㄣ | wen | ㄨㄣ |
| yang | ㄧㄤ | wang | ㄨㄤ |
| ying | ㄧㄥ | weng | ㄨㄥ |
| yu | ㄩ | yue | ㄩㄝ |
| yuan | ㄩㄢ | yun | ㄩㄣ |
| yong | ㄩㄥ | | |

### 3. Pinyin short forms: iu, ui, un

Pinyin drops a vowel in three finals after an initial. Zhuyin keeps it:

| Pinyin | Zhuyin | Example |
|---|---|---|
| -iu (= iou) | ㄧㄡ | 六 ㄌㄧㄡˋ *liù* (six) |
| -ui (= uei) | ㄨㄟ | 對 ㄉㄨㄟˋ *duì* (correct) |
| -un (= uen) | ㄨㄣ | 春 ㄔㄨㄣ *chūn* (spring) |

### 4. ü after j, q, x

Pinyin drops the dots of **ü** after j, q, x (and y), so *ju* looks like it has a "u". Zhuyin always shows ㄩ:

| Pinyin | Zhuyin | Example |
|---|---|---|
| ju | ㄐㄩ | 句 ㄐㄩˋ (sentence) |
| qu | ㄑㄩ | 去 ㄑㄩˋ (to go) |
| xue | ㄒㄩㄝ | 學 ㄒㄩㄝˊ (to study) |
| jun | ㄐㄩㄣ | 軍 ㄐㄩㄣ (army) |
| xiong | ㄒㄩㄥ | 熊 ㄒㄩㄥˊ (bear) |

After n and l, both systems show it: 女 ㄋㄩˇ *nǚ* (woman), 綠 ㄌㄩˋ *lǜ* (green).

### 5. ong and iong

Pinyin **-ong** is ㄨㄥ, and **-iong** is ㄩㄥ:

- 東 ㄉㄨㄥ *dōng* (east), 中 ㄓㄨㄥ *zhōng* (middle)
- 窮 ㄑㄩㄥˊ *qióng* (poor)

### 6. Pinyin e has three Zhuyin forms

| Pinyin | Zhuyin | Example |
|---|---|---|
| e (alone or after an initial) | ㄜ | 喝 ㄏㄜ *hē* (to drink) |
| ie, üe | ㄧㄝ, ㄩㄝ | 謝 ㄒㄧㄝˋ *xiè* (to thank) |
| ei, en, eng | ㄟ, ㄣ, ㄥ (one symbol each) | 黑 ㄏㄟ *hēi* (black), 很 ㄏㄣˇ *hěn* (very) |

## Look-alike symbols

| Pair | Tell them apart |
|---|---|
| **ㄛ** o / **ㄜ** e | ㄜ has an extra short stroke on top |
| **ㄣ** en / **ㄥ** eng | ㄣ ends with a hook; ㄥ ends with a flat stroke. The sounds are also easy to mix up, so learn them as a pair. |
| **ㄢ** an / **ㄤ** ang | same issue: -n vs -ng. ㄤ comes from 尢 |

## Typing with Zhuyin

The standard Zhuyin keyboard (大千 layout) puts **one symbol on each key**. You type the symbols of a syllable in order, then the tone key, and choose the character from a list.

| Key row | Symbols, left to right |
|---|---|
| 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8 9 0 - | ㄅ ㄉ ˇ ˋ ㄓ ˊ ˙ ㄚ ㄞ ㄢ ㄦ |
| q w e r t y u i o p | ㄆ ㄊ ㄍ ㄐ ㄔ ㄗ ㄧ ㄛ ㄟ ㄣ |
| a s d f g h j k l ; | ㄇ ㄋ ㄎ ㄑ ㄕ ㄘ ㄨ ㄜ ㄠ ㄤ |
| z x c v b n m , . / | ㄈ ㄌ ㄏ ㄒ ㄖ ㄙ ㄩ ㄝ ㄡ ㄥ |

- **Space** = 1st tone (and confirms the syllable).
- The keyboard follows the recitation order: **ㄅㄆㄇㄈ** go straight down the first column (1, q, a, z), **ㄉㄊㄋㄌ** down the second (2, w, s, x).
- Example: 你 = **s u 3** (ㄋ ㄧ ˇ).

## Learning tips

- **Recite in groups**: *bo po mo fo, de te ne le, ge ke he, ji qi xi, zhi chi shi ri, zi ci si*, then the medials and finals. This is how Taiwanese children learn it.
- **Learn sounds, not letters**: each symbol has exactly one sound, so learn it straight from audio.
- **Read children's books with Zhuyin** beside the characters to practise both at once.
