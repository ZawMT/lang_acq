# Measure Words (量詞)

A number or 這/那/哪 can't go straight before a noun. A **measure word** (量詞 ㄌㄧㄤˋ ㄘˊ *liàngcí*) must go in between:

> number / 這 / 那 / 哪 + **measure word** + noun

> 三**本**書 (three books), 這**個**人 (this person), 哪**張**票? (which ticket?)

三書 ✗, 這書 (only in fixed or written styles).

The measure word usually describes the **shape or type** of the thing.

**個 is the safe fallback**: when you don't know the right measure word, 個 is usually understood, though it can sound less natural.

## Containers and units

Containers and units work as measure words too:

| | Examples |
|---|---|
| containers | 一**杯**水 (a glass of water), 一**瓶**酒 (a bottle of wine), 一**碗**飯 (a bowl of rice), 一**盒**餅乾 (a box of biscuits) |
| units | 一**公斤** (a kilo), 一**公尺** (a metre), 兩**個小時** (two hours) |

## Measure word without the noun

When it's clear what you mean, the noun drops and the measure word stands for it:

> 我要這**個**。 (I want this one.)
> 你要哪**本**？ (Which one (book) do you want?)
> 給我兩**杯**。 (Give me two (cups).)

## Verbal measures: how many times

Some measure words count **actions**, and go **after** the verb:

| Measure | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **次** ㄘˋ *cì* | times, occasions | 我去過兩**次**。 (I've been twice.) |
| **遍** ㄅㄧㄢˋ *biàn* | times, start to finish | 請再說一**遍**。 (Please say it once more.) |
| **下** ㄒㄧㄚˋ *xià* | a quick action | 等一**下** (wait a moment), 看一**下** (take a look) |

## Not counting: 一些, 一點

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 一些 | some (countable or not) | 一些人 (some people) |
| 一點(兒) | a little | 一點水 (a little water) |

## Traps

- **兩, not 二**, before measure words: **兩**個人 (two people), 二個人 ✗. See [Numbers and Time](./08_numbers_time.md).
- **No 們 with numbers**: 三個學生 (three students), 三個學生們 ✗.
- Measure words vary by region: mainland China often uses 條 for dogs (一條狗); Taiwan uses 隻.
