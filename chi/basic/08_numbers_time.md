## Building bigger numbers

Chinese numbers are completely **regular**: just say the parts.

| Number | Chinese | Literally |
|---|---|---|
| 11 | 十一 | ten-one |
| 20 | 二十 | two-ten |
| 35 | 三十五 | three-ten-five |
| 100 | 一百 ㄅㄞˇ *bǎi* | one hundred |
| 1,000 | 一千 ㄑㄧㄢ *qiān* | one thousand |
| 10,000 | 一萬 ㄨㄢˋ *wàn* | one **ten-thousand** |
| 100,000,000 | 一億 ㄧˋ *yì* | one hundred-million |

**Big trap: Chinese counts in units of 萬 (10,000)**, not thousands:

| Number | Chinese |
|---|---|
| 100,000 | 十萬 (ten 萬) |
| 1,000,000 | 一百萬 (a hundred 萬) |
| 10,000,000 | 一千萬 |

### Zeros and 一

- A gap in the middle is read as **零** once: 105 = 一百**零**五, 1,005 = 一千**零**五.
- 10–19 start with 十: 十五 (15). But inside a larger number, say 一十: 115 = 一百**一十**五.

## 二 vs 兩

| Use | Example |
|---|---|
| **兩** before a measure word | **兩**個人 (two people), **兩**本書 |
| **二** in counting, ordinals, decimals, phone numbers | 一、**二**、三; 第**二** (second); 十**二** (12) |
| **二** in the tens and ones place of larger numbers | 十二個人 (twelve people) |
| either before 百 | 兩百 / 二百 (200) |
| usually **兩** before 千, 萬, 億 | 兩千 (2,000), 兩萬 (20,000) |

## Tone change of 一

一 changes its tone depending on what follows:

| | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| alone, counting, ordinals, end of a word | 1st: *yī* | 一, 第一 (first), 十一 |
| before a 4th tone (or 個) | 2nd: *yí* | 一個 *yí ge*, 一定 *yídìng* |
| before 1st, 2nd, 3rd tones | 4th: *yì* | 一天 *yì tiān*, 一年 *yì nián*, 一百 *yì bǎi* |

In phone and room numbers, mainland China often says **幺** *yāo* for 1, to avoid confusion with 七.

## Ordinals, fractions, percentages

| | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| ordinal | **第** ㄉㄧˋ *dì* + number | 第一 (first), 第三課 (Lesson 3) |
| fraction | B 分之 A | 三分之一 (1/3) |
| percent | 百分之 + number | 百分之五十 (50%) |
| decimal | 點 *diǎn* | 3.5 = 三點五 |

## Dates: big to small

> 2026年11月25日 → 二〇二六年十一月二十五日

- **Years** are read **digit by digit**: 2026 = 二〇二六 *èr líng èr liù* 年.
- **Day**: 日 ㄖˋ *rì* in writing, **號** ㄏㄠˋ *hào* in speech: 二十五號.
- **Taiwan** also uses the **Minguo (Republic) calendar** (民國), counting from 1912: 2026 = 民國115年. It appears on official documents and receipts.

### Days of the week

| Day | Chinese |
|---|---|
| Monday – Saturday | 星期一 … 星期六 |
| Sunday | 星期日 / 星期天 |

星期 ㄒㄧㄥ ㄑㄧ *xīngqī* can also be **禮拜** *lǐbài* (spoken) or **週** *zhōu* (written, Taiwan): 週一 (Mon).

### Relative time

| Before | Now | After |
|---|---|---|
| 前天 (day before yesterday), 昨天 (yesterday) | 今天 (today) | 明天 (tomorrow), 後天 (day after tomorrow) |
| 去年 (last year) | 今年 (this year) | 明年 (next year) |
| 上個月 (last month) | 這個月 | 下個月 (next month) |
| 上星期 (last week) | 這星期 | 下星期 (next week) |

上 = last, 下 = next: past is "up", future is "down".

## Clock time

| Word | Zhuyin | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **點** | ㄉㄧㄢˇ | diǎn | o'clock |
| **分** | ㄈㄣ | fēn | minute |
| **半** | ㄅㄢˋ | bàn | half |
| **刻** | ㄎㄜˋ | kè | quarter (more common in mainland China) |
| **差** | ㄔㄚ | chà | to (before the hour) |

| Time | Chinese |
|---|---|
| 2:00 | **兩**點 (not 二點) |
| 3:30 | 三點半 |
| 4:15 | 四點十五分 / 四點一刻 |
| 5:55 | 五點五十五分 / 差五分六點 |

Part of day goes **before** the time: 早上八點 (8 a.m.), 下午三點 (3 p.m.), 晚上十點 (10 p.m.).

## Asking numbers

| Question | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **幾** ㄐㄧˇ *jǐ* | small numbers, + measure word | 你有**幾**個哥哥？ (How many older brothers do you have?) |
| **多少** ㄉㄨㄛ ㄕㄠˇ *duōshǎo* | any number | 這個**多少**錢？ (How much is this?) |
