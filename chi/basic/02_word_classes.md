# Word Classes (詞類)

Chinese words **never change form**. There are no conjugations, no plural endings, no case endings: 吃 *chī* (eat) is always 吃, whether "ate", "eating", or "will eat". A word's class shows in **where it stands** and **what it does** in the sentence, not in its shape.

Chinese grammar divides words into two big groups:

| Group | Zhuyin | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **實詞** | ㄕˊ ㄘˊ | shící | content words: carry meaning (nouns, verbs, adjectives…) |
| **虛詞** | ㄒㄩ ㄘˊ | xūcí | function words: carry grammar (prepositions, particles…) |

In Taiwan, pronouns are often called **代名詞** and interjections **感嘆詞**.

## 名詞: nouns

- **No plural**: 書 = book or books. Number comes from context or a number + measure word: 三本書 (three books).
- **No articles**: no "a" or "the". 這 (this) / 那 (that) or word order show what's known (see [Word Order](./04_word_order.md)).
- **們** ˙ㄇㄣ *men* marks plural only for **people**, and not when there's a number: 學生們 (the students), but 三個學生 (三個學生們 ✗).
- **Time and place words** are nouns too and can be subjects: 今天星期一。 (Today is Monday.)

## 動詞: verbs

Verbs don't change for person or time. **Aspect** is shown by particles after the verb (see [Aspect](./12_aspect.md)):

| | Example |
|---|---|
| plain | 我**吃**飯。 (I eat.) |
| completed | 我**吃了**飯。 (I ate.) |
| experienced | 我**吃過**。 (I've had it before.) |
| in progress | 我**在吃**飯。 (I'm eating.) |

Many verbs take **complements** that add result or direction: 吃**完** (finish eating), 走**進來** (walk in). See [Complements](./14_complements.md).

**Modal verbs** (會, 能, 可以, 要, 應該…) come before the main verb (see [Modal Verbs](./17_modal_verbs.md)).

## 形容詞: adjectives act like verbs

Adjectives are the **predicate** by themselves. **No 是** (be):

> 她很高。 → *Tā hěn gāo.* (She is tall.) 她是高 ✗

They behave like verbs in other ways too: they take 不 (不高), A-not-A questions (高不高?), and 了 for change (天黑了, it's got dark). See [Adjectives](./06_adjectives.md).

## 數詞 + 量詞: numbers need measure words

A number or 這/那 can't go straight before a noun; a **measure word** goes in between:

> 一**本**書 (one book), 三**個**人 (three people), 這**張**桌子 (this table)

See [Measure Words](./07_measure_words.md).

## 副詞: adverbs

Adverbs go **before the verb or adjective**, after the subject: 我**也**去 (I'm going too), 他們**都**是學生 (they're all students). Small adverbs like 就, 才, 還, 都 carry a lot of meaning (see [Adverbs](./18_adverbs.md)).

## 介詞: prepositions come from verbs

Chinese has **pre**positions (before the noun), and most started life as verbs. A phrase with a preposition goes **before** the main verb:

> 我**在家**吃飯。 → *Wǒ zài jiā chī fàn.* (I eat at home.)

See [Prepositions](./15_prepositions.md).

## 助詞: particles

Small, unstressed words, usually neutral tone, that carry grammar:

| Type | Particles | See |
|---|---|---|
| structural | 的, 地, 得 (all *de*) | [的, 地, 得](./13_de_de_de.md) |
| aspect | 了, 過, 著 | [Aspect](./12_aspect.md) |
| sentence-final | 嗎, 呢, 吧, 啊, 了 | [Sentence-final Particles](./21_sentence_final_particles.md) |

## 兼類: one word, several classes

Because words don't change form, **the same word can belong to more than one class**. Its position tells you which:

| Word | As… | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **在** | verb (be at) | 他**在**家。 (He's at home.) |
| | preposition (at) | 他**在**家吃飯。 (He eats at home.) |
| | adverb (in progress) | 他**在**吃飯。 (He's eating.) |
| **給** | verb (give) | 我**給**你一本書。 (I'll give you a book.) |
| | preposition (for, to) | 我**給**你打電話。 (I'll call you.) |
| **好** | adjective (good) | 很**好**。 (Very good.) |
| | adverb (so, very) | **好**冷! (So cold!) |
| **學習** | verb (study) | 我在**學習**中文。 (I'm studying Chinese.) |
| | noun (study) | **學習**很重要。 (Studying is important.) |

**Tip:** when a word looks like it's in the "wrong" class, check its position. Before a noun → probably modifying it; before a verb → probably a preposition or adverb; after a verb → probably a complement or particle.
