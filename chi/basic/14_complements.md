# Complements (補語)

A **complement** (補語 ㄅㄨˇ ㄩˇ *bǔyǔ*) comes **right after a verb** and tells the **outcome**: the result, direction, possibility, degree, or amount. Complements are one of the most typical, and most difficult, parts of Chinese grammar.

| Type | Zhuyin | Pinyin | Adds | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **result** 結果補語 | ㄐㄧㄝˊ ㄍㄨㄛˇ | jiéguǒ | what the action achieved | 看**完** (finish reading) |
| **direction** 趨向補語 | ㄑㄩ ㄒㄧㄤˋ | qūxiàng | which way it moves | 走**進來** (walk in) |
| **potential** 可能補語 | ㄎㄜˇ ㄋㄥˊ | kěnéng | can / can't achieve | 看**得懂** / 看**不懂** (can / can't understand) |
| **degree** 程度補語 | ㄔㄥˊ ㄉㄨˋ | chéngdù | how well, how much | 跑**得很快** (run fast) |
| **quantity** 數量補語 | ㄕㄨˋ ㄌㄧㄤˋ | shùliàng | how long, how many times | 睡**了八個小時** (slept 8 hours) |

## The key idea: trying vs succeeding

Chinese verbs often describe only the **attempt**. The **result** needs a complement:

| Attempt | Result |
|---|---|
| 看 (look) | 看**見** / 看**到** (see) |
| 聽 (listen) | 聽**見** (hear), 聽**懂** (understand) |
| 找 (look for) | 找**到** (find) |
| 學 (study) | 學**會** (learn, master) |

> 我**找**了半天，可是沒**找到**。 → *Wǒ zhǎo le bàntiān, kěshì méi zhǎo dào.* (I looked for ages but didn't find it.)
> 我**看**了，可是沒**看懂**。 → (I read it, but didn't understand it.)

## 1. Result complements

Verb + result (a verb or adjective):

| Result | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **完** | finished | 吃完, 寫完, 看完 |
| **好** | done properly, ready | 準備好 (get ready), 做好 |
| **到** | reached, achieved | 找到 (find), 買到 (manage to buy), 聽到 (hear) |
| **見** | perceived | 看見 (see), 聽見 (hear) |
| **懂** | understood | 聽懂, 看懂 |
| **會** | mastered | 學會 (learn to do) |
| **錯** | wrongly | 寫錯 (write wrongly), 打錯 (dial wrongly) |
| **對** | correctly | 說對 (say correctly), 猜對 (guess right) |
| **飽** | full | 吃飽 (eat your fill) |
| **開** | open, apart | 打開 (open), 離開 (leave) |
| **住** | firmly, fixed | 記住 (remember), 停住 (stop) |
| **乾淨** | clean | 洗乾淨 (wash clean) |
| **清楚** | clear | 說清楚 (explain clearly), 看清楚 (see clearly) |

> 我**吃飽**了。 (I'm full.)
> 你**打錯**電話了。 (You've dialled the wrong number.)

**Negative:** 沒 + verb + result: 我**沒聽懂**。 (I didn't understand.)

## 2. Direction complements

**來** = toward the speaker, **去** = away from the speaker.

| | + 來 (toward me) | + 去 (away) |
|---|---|---|
| 上 (up) | 上來 | 上去 |
| 下 (down) | 下來 | 下去 |
| 進 (in) | 進來 | 進去 |
| 出 (out) | 出來 | 出去 |
| 回 (back) | 回來 | 回去 |
| 過 (over) | 過來 | 過去 |
| 起 (up) | 起來 | — |

A movement verb + these:

> 他**走進來**了。 → *Tā zǒu jìnlái le.* (He walked in.)
> 你**跑出去**吧。 → (Run out.)
> 請**拿回來**。 → (Please bring it back.)

**Place objects** go **before 來/去**:

> 他走進**教室**來了。 (He walked into the classroom.)
> 我回**家**去。 (I'm going back home.)

### Extended meanings

Many direction complements have abstract meanings:

| Complement | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **起來** | start; when looked at | 笑**起來** (start laughing), 看**起來**很好吃 (it looks tasty) |
| **想起來** | recall | 我**想起來**了！ (I remember now!) |
| **下去** | continue | 說**下去** (keep talking) |
| **下來** | come to a stop; record | 停**下來** (stop), 寫**下來** (write down) |
| **出來** | come out, figure out | 想**出來** (think of), 看**出來** (make out) |

## 3. Potential complements: can / can't

Put **得** (can) or **不** (can't) **between** the verb and its result or direction:

| Verb + result | Can | Can't |
|---|---|---|
| 看懂 | 看**得**懂 | 看**不**懂 |
| 吃完 | 吃**得**完 | 吃**不**完 |
| 買到 | 買**得**到 | 買**不**到 |
| 聽清楚 | 聽**得**清楚 | 聽**不**清楚 |
| 進去 | 進**得**去 | 進**不**去 |

> 這麼多菜，我**吃不完**。 → (So much food, I can't finish it.)
> 老師說的話你**聽得懂**嗎？ → (Can you understand what the teacher says?)

Fixed potential forms:

| Expression | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **來得及 / 來不及** | have / not have enough time |
| **買不起** | can't afford |
| **受不了** | can't stand |
| **吃不了** | can't eat it (all) |
| **對不起** | sorry (lit. can't face you) |

### 看不懂 vs 不能看

| | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 我**看不懂**。 | I can't understand it (I tried; the result didn't come). |
| 我**不能看**。 | I'm not allowed / not able to look. |

The potential form is the **natural** way to say "can't achieve the result". 我不能看懂 ✗.

## 4. Degree complements

Verb or adjective + **得** + description (see [的, 地, 得](./13_de_de_de.md)):

> 他跑**得很快**。 (He runs fast.)
> 她中文說**得非常好**。 (She speaks Chinese very well.)

Short forms without 得:

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| adj + **極了** | 好**極了** (excellent) |
| adj + **死了** | 累**死了** (dead tired), 熱**死了** (boiling) |
| adj + **多了** | 好**多了** (much better) |
| adj + **得很** | 好**得很** (very good) |

## 5. Quantity complements

**How long** or **how many times**, after the verb:

> 我睡了**八個小時**。 (I slept eight hours.)
> 這部電影我看了**兩遍**。 (I've watched this film twice.)
> 請等**一下**。 (Please wait a moment.)

With an object, repeat the verb or put the object between:

> 我**學中文學了**三年。 / 我學了三年**的**中文。 (I studied Chinese for three years.)
