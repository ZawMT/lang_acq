# Connecting Clauses (複句)

Chinese often links two clauses with a **pair of words**: one in each clause. Where English says "*Although* it's expensive, I'll buy it", Chinese says "*Although* it's expensive, *but* I'll buy it":

> **雖然**很貴，**但是**我要買。 → *Suīrán hěn guì, dànshì wǒ yào mǎi.*

Both halves can appear, and often do. Often one half can be dropped in speech.

## The main pairs

| Pair | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **因為…所以…** | because… so… | **因為**下雨，**所以**我沒去。 (Because it rained, I didn't go.) |
| **雖然…但是/可是…** | although… (but)… | **雖然**很累，**可是**很開心。 (Tired, but happy.) |
| **如果/要是…就…** | if… then… | **如果**你去，我**就**去。 (If you go, I'll go.) |
| **只要…就…** | as long as… | **只要**努力，**就**會成功。 (As long as you work hard, you'll succeed.) |
| **只有…才…** | only if… | **只有**多練習，**才**能說好。 (Only with lots of practice can you speak well.) |
| **一…就…** | as soon as… | 我**一**到家**就**睡覺。 (I go to sleep as soon as I get home.) |
| **不但…而且/還…** | not only… but also… | 他**不但**會說中文，**而且**會說日文。 (He speaks not only Chinese but Japanese too.) |
| **即使/就算…也…** | even if… | **即使**下雨，我**也**要去。 (Even if it rains, I'm going.) |
| **既然…就…** | since… then… | **既然**你來了，**就**一起吃吧。 (Since you're here, let's eat together.) |
| **不是…就是…** | either… or… | 他**不是**在看書，**就是**在睡覺。 (He's either reading or sleeping.) |
| **不是…而是…** | not… but rather… | 我**不是**不想去，**而是**沒時間。 (It's not that I don't want to go — I just don't have time.) |
| **除了…以外，還/也…** | besides… also… | **除了**中文**以外**，我**還**學日文。 (Besides Chinese, I'm also studying Japanese.) |
| **除了…以外，都…** | except… | **除了**他**以外**，大家**都**來了。 (Everyone came except him.) |

For more "if" words (要是, 假如, 要不是), see [要是](../intermediate/grammar/y.md).

## Sequence

| Pattern | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **先…再/然後…** | first… then… | 我們**先**吃飯，**然後**去看電影。 (We'll eat first, then see a film.) |
| **…以後** | after… | 下課**以後**，我去打工。 (After class I go to my part-time job.) |
| **…以前** | before… | 睡覺**以前**，我刷牙。 (I brush my teeth before bed.) |
| **…的時候** | when… | 我小**的時候**住在台南。 (When I was small I lived in Tainan.) |

**Trap:** 以後, 以前, 的時候 go **at the end** of the clause: 下課以後 (after class), not 以後下課 ✗.

## At the same time

| Pattern | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **一邊…一邊…** | while (two actions) | 他**一邊**吃飯**一邊**看電視。 (He watches TV while eating.) |
| **又…又…** | both… and… (qualities) | 這家店**又**便宜**又**好吃。 (This place is both cheap and good.) |

## Choices: or

| Word | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **還是** | in **questions** | 你喝茶**還是**咖啡？ (Tea or coffee?) |
| **或者 / 或是** | in **statements** | 星期六**或者**星期天都可以。 (Saturday or Sunday are both fine.) |

## Where the connectors go

- **Same subject** in both clauses: the first connector can go **after** the subject:
  > 我**因為**生病，**所以**沒去上課。 (Because I was ill, I didn't go to class.)
- **Different subjects**: the first connector goes **before** the subject:
  > **因為**我生病了，**所以**他替我去。
- **就, 才, 也, 還, 都** are adverbs: they go **after** the subject in the second clause, never before it:
  > 如果你去，我**就**去。 ✓ / 如果你去，就我去 ✗

## Joining without connectors

In speech, clauses are often just placed side by side; the logic comes from context:

> 下雨了，不去了。 (It's raining, so I'm not going.)
> 你不去，我去。 (If you won't go, I will.)
