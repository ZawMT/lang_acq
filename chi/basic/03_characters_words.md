# Characters and Words (字與詞)

| Term | Zhuyin | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **字** | ㄗˋ | zì | a **character**: one written symbol, one syllable |
| **詞** | ㄘˊ | cí | a **word**: one or more characters with one meaning |

Almost every character is **one syllable** and carries **one piece of meaning**. Most modern words are made of **two characters**:

| Word | Characters | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 書 | 書 (book) | book (one character) |
| 電腦 | 電 (electric) + 腦 (brain) | computer |
| 朋友 | 朋 (friend) + 友 (friend) | friend |
| 圖書館 | 圖 (picture) + 書 (book) + 館 (hall) | library |

So learning characters is like learning **building blocks**: once you know 電 (electric), you can guess 電話 (phone), 電視 (TV), 電影 (film), 電梯 (lift).

## How characters are built

Traditionally there are six types (六書). Four matter most:

| Type | Zhuyin | Pinyin | How it works | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **象形** pictograph | ㄒㄧㄤˋ ㄒㄧㄥˊ | xiàngxíng | a picture of the thing | 日 (sun), 月 (moon), 山 (mountain), 人 (person), 木 (tree) |
| **指事** indicative | ㄓˇ ㄕˋ | zhǐshì | a sign marking an idea | 上 (up), 下 (down), 本 (root: a mark at the bottom of 木) |
| **會意** compound idea | ㄏㄨㄟˋ ㄧˋ | huìyì | two meanings combined | 休 (rest) = 人 + 木 (person leaning on a tree); 明 (bright) = 日 + 月 |
| **形聲** sound + meaning | ㄒㄧㄥˊ ㄕㄥ | xíngshēng | one part hints at the **meaning**, one at the **sound** | 媽 (mother) = 女 (woman) + 馬 *mǎ* (sound) |

**形聲 characters are the large majority** (about 80% or more). The sound part is only a hint, since pronunciations have changed over 2,000+ years:

| Sound part | Characters |
|---|---|
| 馬 mǎ | 媽 mā (mother), 嗎 ma (question), 罵 mà (scold), 碼 mǎ (code) |
| 青 qīng | 清 qīng (clear), 請 qǐng (please), 晴 qíng (sunny), 情 qíng (feeling) |

## 部首: radicals

The **radical** (部首 ㄅㄨˋ ㄕㄡˇ *bùshǒu*) is the part used to sort characters in a dictionary, usually the meaning part. Traditional dictionaries use **214** radicals. Many change shape at the side of a character:

| Radical | At the side | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| 水 | 氵 | water | 河 (river), 海 (sea), 洗 (wash) |
| 手 | 扌 | hand | 打 (hit), 拿 (take), 找 (look for) |
| 人 | 亻 | person | 你 (you), 他 (he), 住 (live) |
| 心 | 忄 | heart, feeling | 想 (think), 忙 (busy), 快 (fast; happy) |
| 口 | 口 | mouth | 吃 (eat), 喝 (drink), 叫 (call) |
| 言 | 言 | speech | 說 (speak), 話 (words), 請 (please) |
| 木 | 木 | tree, wood | 林 (woods), 桌 (table), 椅 (chair) |
| 艸 | 艹 | grass, plants | 花 (flower), 茶 (tea), 菜 (vegetable) |
| 金 | 金 | metal | 錢 (money), 鐵 (iron), 銀 (silver) |
| 火 | 灬 (at the bottom) | fire | 熱 (hot), 點 (dot), 煮 (cook) |

## Strokes (筆畫)

Characters are written in a set **stroke order**. The main rules:

1. top to bottom: 三
2. left to right: 川
3. horizontal before vertical: 十
4. outside before inside, but close the box last: 回
5. middle before the sides when they're symmetrical: 小

Correct order matters for handwriting recognition on phones and for writing neatly.

## How words are built

Chinese makes new words mostly by **combining characters**:

| Pattern | Examples |
|---|---|
| modifier + head | 火車 (fire + vehicle = train), 手機 (hand + machine = mobile phone), 飛機 (fly + machine = plane) |
| two similar meanings | 朋友 (friend), 學習 (study), 道路 (road) |
| two opposites → the whole idea | 大小 (big + small = size), 開關 (open + close = switch), 東西 (east + west = thing) |
| verb + object | 吃飯 (eat + rice = have a meal), 睡覺 (sleep), 唱歌 (sing) |
| verb + result | 打破 (hit + broken = break), 提高 (lift + high = raise) |

A few characters work like **prefixes and suffixes**:

| Affix | Use | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| 老 | prefix (family order, some animals) | 老師 (teacher), 老虎 (tiger), 老大 (eldest) |
| 第 | ordinal prefix | 第一 (first) |
| 子 | noun suffix | 桌子 (table), 椅子 (chair), 孩子 (child) |
| 頭 | noun suffix | 木頭 (wood), 石頭 (stone) |
| 家 | -ist, expert | 科學家 (scientist), 作家 (writer) |
| 們 | plural of people | 我們 (we), 老師們 (the teachers) |
| 化 | -ize | 現代化 (modernize) |

**Verb + object words can split:** 吃飯 → 吃**了**飯, 吃**一點**飯; 睡覺 → 睡**了八小時的**覺. This is very common, and a classic learner trap.

## Traditional and simplified characters

| | 繁體字 ㄈㄢˊ ㄊㄧˇ ㄗˋ *fántǐzì* | 簡體字 ㄐㄧㄢˇ ㄊㄧˇ ㄗˋ *jiǎntǐzì* |
|---|---|---|
| used in | Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macau | mainland China, Singapore |
| history | the historical forms | official simplification in the 1950s–60s |

Taiwan officially calls them **正體字** (*zhèngtǐzì*, "standard characters").

| Traditional | Simplified | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 學 | 学 | study |
| 國 | 国 | country |
| 說 | 说 | speak |
| 書 | 书 | book |
| 電 | 电 | electric |
| 愛 | 爱 | love |
| 車 | 车 | vehicle |
| 門 | 门 | door |
| 龍 | 龙 | dragon |

Simplified radicals change too: 言 → 讠 (說 → 说), 金 → 钅 (錢 → 钱), 糸 → 纟 (紅 → 红).

**Trap:** sometimes **one simplified character** stands for **several traditional ones**:

| Simplified | Traditional |
|---|---|
| 发 | 發 (send out, 發財) / 髮 (hair, 頭髮) |
| 后 | 後 (after) / 后 (queen) |
| 干 | 乾 (dry) / 幹 (do) / 干 (shield) |

## How many characters?

- About **2,500–3,000** characters cover around 99% of everyday reading.
- Taiwan's standard list of common characters has **4,808**.
- Learn characters inside **words**, not one by one: the word gives the meaning, the character gives the building block.
