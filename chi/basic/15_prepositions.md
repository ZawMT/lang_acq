# Prepositions (介詞)

Chinese prepositions come **before** the noun (在家 = at home), and the whole phrase comes **before the main verb**:

> 我**在家**吃飯。 → *Wǒ zài jiā chī fàn.* (I eat at home.)
> 我吃飯在家 ✗

Most prepositions **were verbs**, and many still are. That's why they're often called **coverbs**: they work like a small verb before the main verb.

| Word | As a verb | As a preposition |
|---|---|---|
| 在 | 他**在**家。 (He's at home.) | 他**在**家睡覺。 (He sleeps at home.) |
| 給 | 我**給**你錢。 (I give you money.) | 我**給**你打電話。 (I'll call you.) |
| 到 | 我**到**了。 (I've arrived.) | 我**到**台北去。 (I'm going to Taipei.) |
| 用 | 我**用**筷子。 (I use chopsticks.) | 我**用**筷子吃飯。 (I eat with chopsticks.) |

## Patterns

### 從…到…: from… to…

> **從**九點**到**五點 (from nine to five)
> **從**台北**到**高雄 (from Taipei to Kaohsiung)

### 從 vs 離

| | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **從** | starting point (movement) | 我**從**家出發。 (I set off from home.) |
| **離** | distance between two points | 我家**離**車站很遠。 (My home is far from the station.) |

### 跟 / 和 + 一起: together with

> 我**跟**朋友**一起**去。 (I'm going with friends.)

### 對 vs 給

| | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **對** | attitude, effect toward | **對**身體不好 (bad for your health) |
| **給** | giving, benefiting, recipient | **給**媽媽寫信 (write to mum) |

## After the verb: where it ends up

在, 到, 給 can also come **after** the verb, showing where something **ends up**:

| Before the verb (setting) | After the verb (result) |
|---|---|
| 他**在**台北工作。 (He works in Taipei.) | 他住**在**台北。 (He lives in Taipei.) |
| — | 我走**到**學校。 (I walked to school.) |
| 我**給**他買了一本書。 (I bought a book for him.) | 我送**給**他一本書。 (I gave him a book.) |

> 把書放**在**桌子上。 (Put the book on the table.)

## Traps

- **跟 / 和 join nouns only**: 我**和**你 (you and I). To join verbs or clauses, use 也, 又, 而且: 他會唱歌，**也**會跳舞。 (He can sing and dance.)
- **Order:** prepositional phrase → verb. Not the English order: 我在公園跑步 (I run in the park), 我跑步在公園 ✗.
- **把** and **被** are prepositions too, but with special rules: see [把 and 被](./16_ba_bei.md).
- **比** (than) is a preposition: see [Comparison](./19_comparison.md).
