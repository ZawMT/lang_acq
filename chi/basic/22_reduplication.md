# Reduplication (重疊)

**Reduplication** (重疊 ㄔㄨㄥˊ ㄉㄧㄝˊ *chóngdié*) means **doubling** a word. Since Chinese has no endings, doubling is one of the few ways to change a word's feel. The effect depends on the word class:

| Word class | Effect | Example |
|---|---|---|
| verb | do it **a bit**, try; softer | 看看 (take a look) |
| adjective | **vivid**, descriptive | 高高的 (nice and tall) |
| noun / measure word | **every** | 天天 (every day) |
| adverb | emphasis, rhythm | 慢慢 (slowly) |

## Verbs: do a little, have a go

| Pattern | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| AA | 看看, 試試, 想想 | take a look, give it a try, think it over |
| A一A | 看一看, 等一等 | (same, a bit more relaxed) |
| A了A | 看了看 | took a quick look (past) |
| ABAB (two-syllable verbs) | 休息休息, 研究研究 | have a rest, look into it a bit |
| AAB (verb + object) | 散散步, 聊聊天, 跳跳舞 | go for a stroll, have a chat, dance a bit |

> 你**看看**這個。 → *Nǐ kànkan zhège.* (Have a look at this.)
> 我**想想**。 → (Let me think.)
> 週末我們去**散散步**吧。 → (Let's go for a stroll at the weekend.)

The second syllable is usually **neutral tone**: 看看 *kànkan*.

- It **softens** requests and suggestions, making them sound casual and polite: 你**試試**看 (Give it a try).
- **Not** with verbs of state or result: 是是, 有有, 喜歡喜歡 ✗.
- **Not** with 著, 過, or ongoing actions: 我在看看 ✗.

## Adjectives: vivid

| Pattern | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| AA(的) | 高高的, 紅紅的, 小小的 | nice and tall, rosy, tiny |
| AABB | 乾乾淨淨, 高高興興, 清清楚楚, 漂漂亮亮 | spotless, cheerfully, crystal clear, all dressed up |
| ABB | 綠油油, 亮晶晶, 熱乎乎 | lush green, sparkling, piping hot |

> 她的臉**紅紅的**。 → (Her cheeks are rosy.)
> 房間打掃得**乾乾淨淨**。 → (The room has been cleaned spotless.)
> 他**高高興興**地回家了。 → (He went home happily.)

- Reduplicated adjectives already carry degree, so **no 很**: 很高高的 ✗.
- **No 不**: 不乾乾淨淨 ✗.
- Often used with 的, 地, or 得: see [的, 地, 得](./13_de_de_de.md).

## Nouns and measure words: every

| Example | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 人人 | everyone |
| 天天 | every day |
| 年年 | every year |
| 家家 | every household |
| 個個 | every one |

> 他**天天**運動。 → (He exercises every day.)
> 這些蘋果**個個**都很甜。 → (Every one of these apples is sweet.)

Usually followed by **都**.

## Family terms

Many **kinship terms** are doubled:

> 爸爸, 媽媽, 哥哥, 姐姐, 弟弟, 妹妹, 爺爺, 奶奶

The second syllable is **neutral tone**: 媽媽 *māma*.

Doubling nouns in other cases is **baby talk**, common in Taiwan when talking to small children, or to sound cute: 車車 (car), 狗狗 (doggy), 飯飯 (food).

## Adverbs

| Example | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 常常 | often |
| 剛剛 | just now |
| 慢慢(地) | slowly |
| 好好(地) | properly, well |
| 偷偷(地) | secretly |

> 你**好好**休息。 → (Get some good rest.)
> 請**慢慢**說。 → (Please speak slowly.)
