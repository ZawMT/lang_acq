# Sentence-final Particles (語氣助詞)

**Sentence-final particles** (語氣助詞 ㄩˇ ㄑㄧˋ ㄓㄨˋ ㄘˊ *yǔqì zhùcí*) come at the **end** of a sentence and show the speaker's **attitude**: asking, suggesting, softening, exclaiming. They're short, unstressed, and mostly **neutral tone**. Spoken Chinese, and Taiwan Mandarin in particular, is full of them.

| Particle | Zhuyin | Pinyin | Main function | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **嗎** | ˙ㄇㄚ | ma | yes/no question | 你好**嗎**？ (How are you?) |
| **呢** | ˙ㄋㄜ | ne | "and…?"; ongoing; softening | 你**呢**？ (And you?) |
| **吧** | ˙ㄅㄚ | ba | suggestion; assumption | 我們走**吧**。 (Let's go.) |
| **啊** | ˙ㄚ | a | exclamation; softening | 好**啊**！ (Sure!) |
| **了** | ˙ㄌㄜ | le | change, new situation | 下雨**了**。 (It's raining now.) |
| **啦** | ˙ㄌㄚ | la | 了 + 啊; casual | 好**啦**！ (OK, OK!) |
| **喔 / 哦** | ㄛ | o | reminder, friendly warning | 要小心**喔**！ (Be careful!) |
| **嘛** | ˙ㄇㄚ | ma | it's obvious | 我就說**嘛**！ (See, I told you!) |
| **耶** | ㄧㄝ | ye | surprise, enthusiasm (Taiwan) | 好漂亮**耶**！ (Wow, so pretty!) |

## 嗎: yes/no question

> 你是台灣人**嗎**？ (Are you Taiwanese?)

Not with question words or A-not-A. See [Questions](./10_questions.md).

## 呢

| Use | Example |
|---|---|
| "and you?", "what about…?" | 我很好，你**呢**？ (I'm fine, and you?) |
| "where's…?" | 我的鑰匙**呢**？ (Where are my keys?) |
| softening a question-word question | 你在想什麼**呢**？ (What are you thinking about?) |
| ongoing action | 他在睡覺**呢**。 (He's sleeping.) |
| "still", "yet" | 我還沒吃**呢**。 (I haven't eaten yet.) |

## 吧

| Use | Example |
|---|---|
| suggestion: let's | 我們走**吧**。 (Let's go.) |
| soft command | 你先吃**吧**。 (Go ahead and eat first.) |
| assumption: I suppose | 你是學生**吧**？ (You're a student, right?) |
| reluctant agreement | 好**吧**。 (Oh, all right.) |

## 啊: feeling

啊 adds **warmth** or **emphasis**. It often changes sound after the previous syllable:

| After | Becomes | Example |
|---|---|---|
| a, e, i, o, ü | **呀** ya | 是**呀** (yes, indeed), 你**呀** |
| u, ao, ou | **哇** wa | 好**哇**, 走**哇** |
| -n | **哪** na | 天**哪** (oh my god), 看**哪** |
| others | 啊 a | 好**啊**! |

| Use | Example |
|---|---|
| exclamation | 好熱**啊**！ (It's so hot!) |
| softening a reply | 好**啊**。 (Sure.) |
| softening a question | 你去哪裡**啊**？ (Where are you off to?) |
| listing | 蘋果**啊**、香蕉**啊**，都有。 (Apples, bananas — we've got everything.) |

## 了: change

Sentence-final 了 = something **has changed** or is **now** so:

> 我餓**了**。 (I'm hungry now.)
> 春天到**了**。 (Spring is here.)
> 我不去**了**。 (I'm not going after all.)

See [Aspect](./12_aspect.md#了-at-the-end-change-new-situation).

## 啦: casual 了 + 啊

> 好**啦**，好**啦**！ (OK, OK!)
> 我走**啦**！ (I'm off!)
> 不是**啦**！ (No, that's not it!)

Very common in Taiwan, where it often just makes speech **casual** or **soft**.

## 喔 / 哦: reminder, realization

| Use | Example |
|---|---|
| friendly reminder or warning | 明天見**喔**！ (See you tomorrow!) / 很燙**喔**！ (Careful, it's hot!) |
| "oh, I see" (alone) | **哦**，原來是這樣。 (Oh, so that's it.) |

## 嘛: obviously

> 這樣才對**嘛**！ (Now that's more like it!)
> 你去**嘛**！ (Oh, just go! — coaxing)

## Taiwan Mandarin

Taiwan speech uses particles heavily. **喔, 耶, 啦, 啊** are everywhere, and also **齁** (*ho*, right?, from Taiwanese Hokkien) and **咧** (*lie*, softening). A sentence without any particle can sound **abrupt or cold** in casual conversation.

## Combining

Particles can stack, usually in this order: **了 → 呢/吧/嗎 → 啊**:

> 他走**了吧**？ (He's left, I suppose?)
> 你吃飯**了嗎**？ (Have you eaten?)
