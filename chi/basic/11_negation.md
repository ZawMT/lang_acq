# Negation (否定)

Chinese has two main negative words, **不** and **沒(有)**, plus **別** for commands.

| Word | Zhuyin | Pinyin | Negates |
|---|---|---|---|
| **不** | ㄅㄨˋ | bù | habits, present, future, will; adjectives; 是; modal verbs |
| **沒(有)** | ㄇㄟˊ (ㄧㄡˇ) | méi(yǒu) | past events (didn't happen); 有; experience (過) |
| **別** | ㄅㄧㄝˊ | bié | commands: don't! |

## 不: won't, don't, isn't

不 negates **will, habits, states, and qualities**:

> 我**不**去。 → *Wǒ bú qù.* (I'm not going / I won't go.)
> 我**不**喝酒。 → *Wǒ bù hē jiǔ.* (I don't drink.)
> 這個**不**貴。 → *Zhège bú guì.* (This isn't expensive.)
> 他**不**是老師。 → *Tā bú shì lǎoshī.* (He isn't a teacher.)
> 我**不**會游泳。 → *Wǒ bú huì yóuyǒng.* (I can't swim.)

## 沒(有): didn't, haven't

沒 negates **what happened** (or didn't), and **有**:

> 我**沒**去。 → *Wǒ méi qù.* (I didn't go.)
> 他**沒**來。 → *Tā méi lái.* (He didn't come / hasn't come.)
> 我**沒有**錢。 → *Wǒ méiyǒu qián.* (I have no money.)
> 我**沒**去過日本。 → *Wǒ méi qù guo Rìběn.* (I've never been to Japan.)

沒 and 沒有 are interchangeable before a verb. Alone as an answer, use **沒有**.

### 不 vs 沒

| | 不 | 沒 |
|---|---|---|
| time | present, future, habits | past, up to now |
| meaning | won't, don't want to, isn't | didn't, hasn't |
| example | 我**不**吃。 (I won't eat / I don't eat it.) | 我**沒**吃。 (I didn't eat / I haven't eaten.) |

**Past habits** still take 不: 我以前**不**喝咖啡。 (I didn't use to drink coffee.)

**Trap: 了 disappears with 沒**:

> 我吃**了**。 → 我**沒**吃。 (not 我沒吃了 ✗)

## 還沒(有)…(呢): not yet

> 我**還沒**吃飯**呢**。 → *Wǒ hái méi chī fàn ne.* (I haven't eaten yet.)
> 他**還沒**來。 → (He's not here yet.)

## 別 / 不要: don't!

> **別**擔心。 → *Bié dānxīn.* (Don't worry.)
> **不要**跑！ → *Bú yào pǎo!* (Don't run!)

別 is shorter and more spoken. **不用** = "no need": 你**不用**來。 (You don't need to come.)

## Tone change of 不

不 is **4th tone** (*bù*), but becomes **2nd tone** (*bú*) before another 4th tone:

| | Example |
|---|---|
| bù (before 1st, 2nd, 3rd tones) | 不吃 *bù chī*, 不來 *bù lái*, 不好 *bù hǎo* |
| **bú** (before 4th tone) | 不去 *bú qù*, 不是 *bú shì*, 不對 *bú duì* |
| neutral (inside A-not-A, complements) | 去不去 *qù bu qù*, 看不懂 *kàn bu dǒng* |

## Position matters

不 negates **what comes right after it**:

| Sentence | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 我們**都不**去。 | None of us is going. |
| 我們**不都**去。 | Not all of us are going. |
| 我**不**在家吃飯。 | I don't eat at home. |
| 我在家**不**吃飯。 | At home, I don't eat. |

With modal verbs, the negative goes **before the modal**: 我**不**想去 (I don't want to go), 我想不去 ✗ (odd).

## Negation in complements

With result complements, 不 goes **in the middle** for "can't":

> 看得懂 (can understand) → 看**不**懂 (can't understand)
> 吃得完 (can finish) → 吃**不**完 (can't finish)

See [Complements](./14_complements.md).

## Double negatives

Two negatives make a **strong positive**:

| Pattern | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 不是不… | it's not that I don't… | 我**不是不**想去。 (It's not that I don't want to go.) |
| 不能不… / 不得不… | can't not = have to | 我**不得不**走了。 (I have no choice but to go.) |
| 沒有…不… | there's no… that doesn't | **沒有**人**不**喜歡他。 (Everyone likes him.) |
