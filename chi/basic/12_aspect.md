# Aspect (動態)

Chinese has **no tense**. The verb doesn't change for past, present, or future. **When** something happens comes from **time words** and context:

> 我**昨天**去學校。 (I went to school yesterday.)
> 我**每天**去學校。 (I go to school every day.)
> 我**明天**去學校。 (I'll go to school tomorrow.)

What Chinese marks instead is **aspect**: how the action is viewed — finished, experienced, ongoing, in progress.

| Marker | Zhuyin | Pinyin | Aspect | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **了** | ˙ㄌㄜ | le | completed | 我買**了**一本書。 (I bought a book.) |
| **過** | ˙ㄍㄨㄛ | guo | experienced (ever) | 我去**過**日本。 (I've been to Japan.) |
| **著** | ˙ㄓㄜ | zhe | ongoing state | 門開**著**。 (The door is open.) |
| **在 / 正在** | ㄗㄞˋ / ㄓㄥˋ ㄗㄞˋ | zài / zhèngzài | in progress | 我**在**吃飯。 (I'm eating.) |

## 了 after the verb: completed

Verb + 了 = the action is **completed**:

> 我買**了**三本書。 → *Wǒ mǎi le sān běn shū.* (I bought three books.)
> 他喝**了**兩杯咖啡。 → (He drank two cups of coffee.)

A bare object after V+了 sounds **unfinished**. Add a number, a description, or a follow-up:

> 我吃了飯。 (sounds incomplete)
> 我吃了**很多**飯。 (I ate a lot.)
> 我吃了飯**就走**。 (I'll leave after I eat.)

V+了 can also be **future** completed: 你下了課來找我。 (Come and see me after class.)

**Negative:** 沒 + verb, and 了 drops: 我**沒**買。 (I didn't buy it.)

## 了 at the end: change, new situation

Sentence-final 了 means **something has changed** or is **now** the case:

> 下雨**了**。 → *Xià yǔ le.* (It's started raining.)
> 我二十歲**了**。 → (I'm twenty now.)
> 我不去**了**。 → (I'm not going after all.)
> 好**了**！ → (Done! / That's enough!)

### Both together: still continuing

V + 了 + duration + 了 = has been… **and still is**:

> 我學**了**三年中文。 (I studied Chinese for three years — finished.)
> 我學**了**三年中文**了**。 (I've been studying Chinese for three years — and still am.)

**Trap: 了 ≠ past tense.** Many past events take **no 了**:

| | Example |
|---|---|
| past habits | 我以前**常**去那裡。 (I used to go there often.) |
| past states | 他以前**是**老師。 (He used to be a teacher.) |
| verbs of thinking, feeling | 我那時候**很喜歡**他。 (I liked him a lot then.) |

## 過: have ever done

V + 過 = the experience happened **at some time** in the past:

> 我去**過**日本。 → *Wǒ qù guo Rìběn.* (I've been to Japan.)
> 你吃**過**臭豆腐嗎？ → (Have you ever eaten stinky tofu?)
> 我**沒**看**過**這部電影。 → (I've never seen this film.)

With 沒, **過 stays** (unlike 了).

### 了 vs 過

| | 了 | 過 |
|---|---|---|
| focus | a specific completed event | experience, ever |
| example | 他去**了**日本。 (He went to Japan — and may still be there.) | 他去**過**日本。 (He's been to Japan — and came back.) |

## 著: ongoing state

V + 著 = a **state** continues, often the result of an action:

> 門開**著**。 → *Mén kāi zhe.* (The door is open.)
> 他穿**著**一件紅外套。 → (He's wearing a red jacket.)
> 牆上掛**著**一張畫。 → (A picture is hanging on the wall.)

**V1 著 V2** = doing V2 **while** in the state of V1:

> 他**坐著**看書。 → (He reads sitting down.)
> 別**站著**吃飯。 → (Don't eat standing up.)

## 在 / 正在: in progress

在 / 正在 + V (+ 呢) = action **in progress** right now:

> 我**在**吃飯。 → *Wǒ zài chī fàn.* (I'm eating.)
> 他**正在**開會**呢**。 → *Tā zhèngzài kāihuì ne.* (He's in a meeting right now.)

正在 stresses "right at this moment". 呢 at the end adds an ongoing, "that's what's happening" feel.

**Negative:** 沒(在): 我**沒在**看電視。 (I'm not watching TV.)

### 在 vs 著

| 在 + V (action) | V + 著 (state) |
|---|---|
| 他**在**穿衣服。 (He's putting on clothes.) | 他穿**著**衣服。 (He's wearing clothes.) |
| 他**在**開門。 (He's opening the door.) | 門開**著**。 (The door is open.) |

## About to happen

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| **快要…了** | **快要**下雨**了**。 (It's about to rain.) |
| **就要…了** | 我們明天**就要**走**了**。 (We're leaving tomorrow.) |
| **要…了** | 火車**要**開**了**。 (The train is about to leave.) |
