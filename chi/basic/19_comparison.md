# Comparison (比較)

## A 比 B + adjective: A is more… than B

**比** ㄅㄧˇ *bǐ* = "than". The adjective goes at the **end**:

> 他**比**我高。 → *Tā bǐ wǒ gāo.* (He's taller than me.)
> 今天**比**昨天冷。 → (Today is colder than yesterday.)
> 台北**比**高雄大。 → (Taipei is bigger than Kaohsiung.)

**Trap: no 很, 非常, 太** in a 比 sentence:

> 他比我很高 ✗

### Adding "even more" or "how much more"

| Pattern | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 比 B **更** + adj | even more | 他比我**更**高。 (He's even taller than me.) |
| 比 B **還** + adj | even more (spoken) | 他比我**還**高。 |
| 比 B + adj + **一點** | a bit more | 他比我高**一點**。 (He's a bit taller.) |
| 比 B + adj + **多了 / 得多** | much more | 他比我高**多了**。 (He's much taller.) |
| 比 B + adj + **amount** | exactly how much | 他比我高**五公分**。 (He's 5 cm taller.) |

### With verbs

When comparing **how** someone does something, use 得:

> 他跑**得比我快**。 / 他**比我**跑得快。 (He runs faster than me.)

## A 沒有 B (那麼) + adjective: A isn't as… as B

The usual **negative** of 比:

> 我**沒有**他高。 → *Wǒ méiyǒu tā gāo.* (I'm not as tall as him.)
> 今天**沒有**昨天**那麼**冷。 → (Today isn't as cold as yesterday.)

那麼 (*nàme*, so) adds "as… as". 這麼 (*zhème*) when the thing is close at hand: 沒有這麼貴 (not this expensive).

### 不比

**不比** means "**not more… than**": often the two are about the same, or it contradicts what someone said:

> 他**不比**我高。 (He's not taller than me — we're about the same.)

## A 跟 B 一樣 (+ adjective): A is the same as B

> 他**跟**我**一樣**高。 → *Tā gēn wǒ yíyàng gāo.* (He's as tall as me.)
> 這個**跟**那個**一樣**。 → (This is the same as that.)
> 這個**跟**那個**不一樣**。 → (This is different from that.)

跟 can be **和**: 我**和**你**一樣**。

## 像…一樣: like

> 她**像**花**一樣**漂亮。 → (She's as beautiful as a flower.)
> 他**像**他爸爸。 → (He looks like his dad.)

## 最: most

> 他是我們班**最**高的。 → *Tā shì wǒmen bān zuì gāo de.* (He's the tallest in our class.)
> 我**最**喜歡夏天。 → (I like summer best.)

## 比較: rather, relatively

**比較** as an adverb = "more (than others)", without naming what it's compared to:

> 這個**比較**好。 → (This one's better.)
> 我**比較**喜歡喝茶。 → (I prefer tea.)

## Change: 越來越 and 越…越…

| Pattern | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **越來越** + adj | more and more | 天氣**越來越**冷了。 (It's getting colder and colder.) |
| **越** A **越** B | the more A, the more B | **越**快**越**好。 (The sooner the better.) / 他**越**說**越**生氣。 (The more he talked, the angrier he got.) |

## Formal: 不如

**A 不如 B** = A isn't as good as B (formal, written):

> 我**不如**他。 (I'm not as good as him.)
> 百聞**不如**一見。 (Seeing once beats hearing a hundred times.)

## Summary

| Meaning | Pattern |
|---|---|
| more than | A **比** B + adj |
| not as… as | A **沒有** B (那麼) + adj |
| the same as | A **跟** B **一樣** (+ adj) |
| like | A **像** B (一樣) |
| most | **最** + adj |
| more and more | **越來越** + adj |
