# Modal Verbs (能願動詞)

**Modal verbs** (能願動詞 ㄋㄥˊ ㄩㄢˋ ㄉㄨㄥˋ ㄘˊ *néngyuàn dòngcí*) come **before the main verb** and add ability, permission, wish, or necessity.

- They're negated with **不**: 不會, 不能, 不想.
- They make A-not-A questions: 會不會？ 能不能？ 可不可以？
- They **don't take** 了, 過, 著.
- They can stand alone as an answer: 你會開車嗎？ → **會**。

## Ability and permission: 會, 能, 可以

| Word | Zhuyin | Pinyin | Main meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **會** | ㄏㄨㄟˋ | huì | can (a **learned skill**) | 我**會**游泳。 (I can swim.) |
| **能** | ㄋㄥˊ | néng | can (**able**, circumstances allow) | 我今天**不能**去。 (I can't go today.) |
| **可以** | ㄎㄜˇ ㄧˇ | kěyǐ | may (**permission**); can | 我**可以**進來嗎？ (May I come in?) |

### 會: learned skill

> 我**會**說中文。 (I can speak Chinese — I've learned it.)
> 他**不會**開車。 (He can't drive — never learned.)

### 能: able, circumstances

> 我感冒了，**不能**去上班。 (I've got a cold and can't go to work.)
> 你一天**能**看完這本書嗎？ (Can you finish this book in a day?)

### 可以: permission

> 這裡**可以**拍照嗎？ (Can I take photos here?)
> 這裡**不可以**抽菸。 (No smoking here.)

As a reply, **不行** (no, not OK) often replaces 不可以.

### The overlap

The three often overlap. In Taiwan, **可以** is used very widely, also for ability. A rough guide:

| | Learned? | Physically / circumstances? | Allowed? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 會 | ✓ | | |
| 能 | | ✓ | (✓) |
| 可以 | | (✓) | ✓ |

> 我**會**游泳，可是今天**不能**游，因為游泳池**不可以**進去。
> (I can swim, but I can't today, because we're not allowed in the pool.)

### 會 = will (likely)

會 also means **probably will**:

> 明天**會**下雨。 (It'll rain tomorrow.)
> 他**不會**生氣的。 (He won't get angry.)

## Wishes and plans

| Word | Zhuyin | Pinyin | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **要** | ㄧㄠˋ | yào | want to, will | 我**要**喝咖啡。 (I want coffee.) |
| **想** | ㄒㄧㄤˇ | xiǎng | would like to | 我**想**去台灣。 (I'd like to go to Taiwan.) |
| **願意** | ㄩㄢˋ ㄧˋ | yuànyì | be willing to | 你**願意**幫我嗎？ (Would you be willing to help me?) |
| **打算** | ㄉㄚˇ ㄙㄨㄢˋ | dǎsuàn | plan to | 我**打算**明年結婚。 (I plan to marry next year.) |

想 is softer than 要. 要 sounds more decided.

## Necessity

| Word | Zhuyin | Pinyin | Meaning | Feel |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **應該** | ㄧㄥ ㄍㄞ | yīnggāi | should | advice, expectation |
| **要** | ㄧㄠˋ | yào | need to | neutral |
| **得** | ㄉㄟˇ | děi | have to | spoken |
| **必須** | ㄅㄧˋ ㄒㄩ | bìxū | must | formal, rules |

> 你**應該**多休息。 (You should rest more.)
> 我**得**走了。 (I have to go.)
> 學生**必須**穿制服。 (Students must wear uniforms.)

應該 also means "probably": 他**應該**到了。 (He should be there by now.)

## Possibility

| Word | Pinyin | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **可能** | kěnéng | may, possibly | 他**可能**不來了。 (He might not come.) |
| **會** | huì | will (likely) | 不**會**吧！ (No way! / Surely not!) |

## Negatives: they don't all match

The negative of a modal isn't always 不 + the same word:

| Positive | Negative | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 會 | 不會 | 我**不會**游泳。 (I can't swim.) |
| 能 | 不能 | 我**不能**去。 (I can't go.) |
| 可以 | 不可以 / 不行 / 不能 | 這裡**不可以**停車。 (No parking here.) |
| 要 (want) | **不想** | 我**不想**去。 (I don't want to go.) |
| 要 / 得 (need) | **不用** | 你**不用**來。 (You don't need to come.) |
| 必須 | **不必** / 不用 | 你**不必**擔心。 (You needn't worry.) |
| 應該 | 不應該 | 你**不應該**這樣說。 (You shouldn't say that.) |

**Trap:** **不要** usually means **"don't!"**, not "don't want": **不要**去！ (Don't go!)
