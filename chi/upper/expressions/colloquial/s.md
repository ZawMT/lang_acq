# 手：插一手

**插一手** ㄔㄚ ㄧˋ ㄕㄡˇ *chā yì shǒu* = to **get involved** in something, usually to **meddle** or **stick your nose in**.

| Character | Zhuyin | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **插** | ㄔㄚ | chā | to insert, stick in |
| **一** | ㄧˋ | yì | one |
| **手** | ㄕㄡˇ | shǒu | hand |

Literally "stick a hand in": putting your hand into someone else's business.

**Tone sandhi:** 一 is ㄧ *yī* on its own, but becomes 4th tone ㄧˋ *yì* before a 3rd tone like 手.

### Feel

Usually **negative**: interference that's unwanted or uninvited. It can also be neutral: "take part," "have a hand in," often to get a **share** of something (profit, influence).

> 這件事你別插一手。 → *Zhè jiàn shì nǐ bié chā yì shǒu.* (Stay out of this.)

> 他想在這筆生意裡插一手。 → *Tā xiǎng zài zhè bǐ shēngyì lǐ chā yì shǒu.* (He wants a piece of this deal.)

> 他想在公司的決策上插一手。 → *Tā xiǎng zài gōngsī de juécè shàng chā yì shǒu.* (He wants a say in the company's decisions.)

It describes the **act** of getting involved. It doesn't say the involvement will hurt you; for that, see [Asking for trouble](#asking-for-trouble) below.

### Type: 慣用語, not 成語

插一手 is a **慣用語** ㄍㄨㄢˋ ㄩㄥˋ ㄩˇ *guànyòngyǔ* (colloquial idiom), not a **成語** ㄔㄥˊ ㄩˇ *chéngyǔ*.

| | 成語 | 慣用語 |
|---|---|---|
| length | almost always 4 characters | often 3, but varies |
| origin | often classical texts, old stories | everyday speech |
| style | literary, fixed | casual, vivid |
| flexible? | no: the form is fixed | yes: words can go inside |

Because it's a 慣用語, it bends in a sentence: 插了一手 (got involved), 插不上手 (can't get involved).

## Same family: 插 + body part

| Expression | Zhuyin | Pinyin | Literal | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **插手** | ㄔㄚ ㄕㄡˇ | chāshǒu | insert hand | to meddle, interfere (the common two-character verb) |
| **插嘴** | ㄔㄚ ㄗㄨㄟˇ | chāzuǐ | insert mouth | to butt into a conversation, interrupt |

> 別插手我的事。 → *Bié chāshǒu wǒ de shì.* (Don't meddle in my affairs.)

> 大人說話，小孩子別插嘴。 → *Dàrén shuōhuà, xiǎo háizi bié chāzuǐ.* (Kids shouldn't butt in when grown-ups are talking.)

## Asking for trouble

For getting involved when it's **obvious it'll cause you pain or trouble**:

| Expression | Zhuyin | Pinyin | Literal | Meaning | Type |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **蹚渾水** | ㄊㄤ ㄏㄨㄣˊ ㄕㄨㄟˇ | tāng húnshuǐ | wade into muddy water | get mixed up in a messy situation you should stay out of | 慣用語 |
| **自找麻煩** | ㄗˋ ㄓㄠˇ ㄇㄚˊ ㄈㄢˊ | zì zhǎo máfán | look for trouble yourself | ask for trouble | ordinary phrase |
| **自討苦吃** | ㄗˋ ㄊㄠˇ ㄎㄨˇ ㄔ | zì tǎo kǔ chī | ask for bitterness to eat | bring hardship on yourself | 成語 |
| **引火燒身** | ㄧㄣˇ ㄏㄨㄛˇ ㄕㄠ ㄕㄣ | yǐn huǒ shāo shēn | draw fire to burn yourself | bring disaster on yourself; backfire | 成語 |

**蹚渾水** is the closest to "getting involved when you know it'll hurt." It's also written 淌渾水 or 趟渾水. Like 插一手, it bends: 蹚這趟渾水 (wade into this muddy water).

> 這事跟你沒關係，別去蹚渾水。 → *Zhè shì gēn nǐ méi guānxi, bié qù tāng húnshuǐ.* (This has nothing to do with you; don't get dragged into the mess.)

> 何必自找麻煩？ → *Hébì zì zhǎo máfán?* (Why ask for trouble?)

> 這麼冷還不穿外套，真是自討苦吃。 → *Zhème lěng hái bù chuān wàitào, zhēn shì zì tǎo kǔ chī.* (Not wearing a coat in this cold is just asking for it.)

> 他公開批評老闆，結果引火燒身。 → *Tā gōngkāi pīpíng lǎobǎn, jiéguǒ yǐn huǒ shāo shēn.* (He criticized the boss publicly and it backfired on him.)

### Together

插一手 = the act; 蹚渾水 = why it's a bad idea:

> 你去插一手，就是蹚渾水。 → *Nǐ qù chā yì shǒu, jiù shì tāng húnshuǐ.* (If you get involved, you're wading into muddy water.)
