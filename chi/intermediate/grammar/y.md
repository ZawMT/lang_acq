# 要：要是

**要是** ㄧㄠˋ ㄕˋ *yàoshì* = **if**. It's the casual, spoken word for "if," placed at the start of the condition clause. (Mainland speech often makes 是 neutral: *yàoshi*.)

> 要是明天下雨，我們就不去了。 → *Yàoshì míngtiān xià yǔ, wǒmen jiù bú qù le.* (If it rains tomorrow, we won't go.)

> 要是你累了，就先休息吧。 → *Yàoshì nǐ lèi le, jiù xiān xiūxi ba.* (If you're tired, take a rest first.)

The result clause often starts with **就** *jiù* (then). **…的話** *de huà* can be added at the end of the condition, especially in speech: 要是你有空的話… (If you're free…).

## 要是…就好了: if only

On its own, 要是 is **not** a wish. The wish comes from the whole pattern **要是…就好了** *yàoshì… jiù hǎo le* ("if…, that would be good"):

> 要是我會說日文就好了。 → *Yàoshì wǒ huì shuō Rìwén jiù hǎo le.* (If only I could speak Japanese.)

> 要是你在這裡就好了。 → *Yàoshì nǐ zài zhèlǐ jiù hǎo le.* (I wish you were here.)

The pattern works with 如果 too (如果…就好了), but 要是 sounds more natural in speech.

## Other words for "if"

| Word | Zhuyin | Pinyin | Feel |
|---|---|---|---|
| **如果** | ㄖㄨˊ ㄍㄨㄛˇ | rúguǒ | the standard "if," neutral |
| **要是** | ㄧㄠˋ ㄕˋ | yàoshì | casual, spoken |
| **假如** | ㄐㄧㄚˇ ㄖㄨˊ | jiǎrú | "suppose," slightly formal |
| **要不是** | ㄧㄠˋ ㄅㄨˊ ㄕˋ | yàobúshì | "if it weren't for…" |

### 假如: suppose

**假如** sets up a **hypothetical**, often imaginary or unlikely. Slightly more formal or written than 要是.

> 假如你中了樂透，你會做什麼？ → *Jiǎrú nǐ zhòngle lètòu, nǐ huì zuò shénme?* (Suppose you won the lottery, what would you do?)

> 假如時間可以倒流，我會更努力讀書。 → *Jiǎrú shíjiān kěyǐ dàoliú, wǒ huì gèng nǔlì dúshū.* (If I could turn back time, I'd study harder.)

### 要不是: if it weren't for

**要不是** looks back at something that **really happened** and imagines it hadn't. The second clause says what would have happened instead.

> 要不是你幫忙，我一定做不完。 → *Yàobúshì nǐ bāngmáng, wǒ yídìng zuò bù wán.* (If it weren't for your help, I'd never have finished.) → you did help

> 要不是下雨，我們早就到了。 → *Yàobúshì xià yǔ, wǒmen zǎo jiù dào le.* (If it hadn't rained, we'd have been there long ago.) → it did rain

**Tone sandhi:** 不 is ㄅㄨˋ *bù*, but becomes 2nd tone ㄅㄨˊ *bú* before a 4th tone like 是.

## Words for "wish" itself

To say "wish" or "hope" directly, use a verb instead of an "if" pattern:

| Word | Zhuyin | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **希望** | ㄒㄧ ㄨㄤˋ | xīwàng | to hope |
| **但願** | ㄉㄢˋ ㄩㄢˋ | dànyuàn | if only, I wish |

> 但願如此。 → *Dànyuàn rúcǐ.* (I hope so.)
