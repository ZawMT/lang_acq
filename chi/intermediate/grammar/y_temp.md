# 要：要 + verb

**要** ㄧㄠˋ *yào* before a verb has several meanings: **want to**, **will / about to**, **need to**. Around it is a family of related words for wanting, needing, insisting, and forbidding.

| Meaning | Example |
|---|---|
| want to | 我**要**喝咖啡。 *Wǒ yào hē kāfēi.* (I want coffee.) |
| be going to, will | 火車**要**開了。 *Huǒchē yào kāi le.* (The train is about to leave.) |
| need to, should | 你**要**多休息。 *Nǐ yào duō xiūxi.* (You need more rest.) |

## Wanting

| Word | Zhuyin | Pinyin | Meaning | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **要** | ㄧㄠˋ | yào | want to, will | neutral, direct |
| **想要** | ㄒㄧㄤˇ ㄧㄠˋ | xiǎng yào | would like to, want | softer than 要 |
| **要不要** | ㄧㄠˋ ㄅㄨˊ ㄧㄠˋ | yào bú yào | do you want to…? | yes/no question form |

> 我想要一杯水。 → *Wǒ xiǎng yào yì bēi shuǐ.* (I'd like a glass of water.)

> 你要不要一起去？ → *Nǐ yào bú yào yìqǐ qù?* (Do you want to come along?)

## Needing

| Word | Zhuyin | Pinyin | Meaning | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **需要** | ㄒㄩ ㄧㄠˋ | xūyào | need to | neutral; also a noun ("need") |
| **得** | ㄉㄟˇ | děi | have to | spoken; this 得 is read *děi*, not *de* |
| **必須** | ㄅㄧˋ ㄒㄩ | bìxū | must | formal, strong (rules, instructions) |
| **一定要** | ㄧˊ ㄉㄧㄥˋ ㄧㄠˋ | yídìng yào | must, be sure to | emphatic |

> 我需要幫忙。 → *Wǒ xūyào bāngmáng.* (I need help.)

> 我得走了。 → *Wǒ děi zǒu le.* (I have to go.)

> 所有乘客必須繫好安全帶。 → *Suǒyǒu chéngkè bìxū jì hǎo ānquándài.* (All passengers must fasten their seatbelts.)

> 你一定要來喔！ → *Nǐ yídìng yào lái o!* (You've got to come!)

### Not needing

| Word | Zhuyin | Pinyin | Meaning | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **不用** | ㄅㄨˊ ㄩㄥˋ | bú yòng | don't need to | the usual spoken negative |
| **不需要** | ㄅㄨˋ ㄒㄩ ㄧㄠˋ | bù xūyào | don't need | more formal |
| **不必** | ㄅㄨˊ ㄅㄧˋ | búbì | needn't | the negative of 必須; formal |

> 不用謝。 → *Bú yòng xiè.* (You're welcome. Lit. no need to thank.)

> 你明天不用來。 → *Nǐ míngtiān bú yòng lái.* (You don't need to come tomorrow.)

## Insisting

| Word | Zhuyin | Pinyin | Meaning | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **非要** | ㄈㄟ ㄧㄠˋ | fēi yào | insist on | stubborn, often against advice |
| **偏要** | ㄆㄧㄢ ㄧㄠˋ | piān yào | deliberately insist on | contrary: does it *because* told not to |
| **硬要** | ㄧㄥˋ ㄧㄠˋ | yìng yào | insist forcibly | pushes against resistance |
| **非…不可** | ㄈㄟ…ㄅㄨˋ ㄎㄜˇ | fēi… bùkě | must, absolutely | necessity or determination |

### 非要: dead set on

**非** ㄈㄟ *fēi* = "not, nothing but," so **非要** ≈ "nothing but want to." The tone is often complaining or puzzled at someone's insistence.

> 外面下大雨，他非要出去。 → *Wàimiàn xià dàyǔ, tā fēi yào chūqù.* (It's pouring outside, but he insists on going out.)

> 我說不用了，她非要請客。 → *Wǒ shuō bú yòng le, tā fēi yào qǐngkè.* (I said there's no need, but she insisted on paying.)

> 你為什麼非要今天去？ → *Nǐ wèishénme fēi yào jīntiān qù?* (Why do you have to go today, of all days?)

### 偏要: doing the opposite on purpose

> 叫他別去，他偏要去。 → *Jiào tā bié qù, tā piān yào qù.* (I told him not to go, so of course he went.)

### 硬要: forcing it

> 他硬要把行李塞進去。 → *Tā yìng yào bǎ xíngli sāi jìnqù.* (He forced the luggage in anyway.)

### 非…不可: absolutely must

Literally "if not…, then not OK." Less about stubbornness, more about **necessity** or **determination**:

> 這件事非你去不可。 → *Zhè jiàn shì fēi nǐ qù bùkě.* (You're the only one who can handle this.)

> 我今天非把報告寫完不可。 → *Wǒ jīntiān fēi bǎ bàogào xiě wán bùkě.* (I absolutely have to finish this report today.)

The two often combine: 他非要去不可。 → *Tā fēi yào qù bùkě.* (He absolutely insists on going.)

### 要 vs 非要

| | 要 | 非要 |
|---|---|---|
| meaning | want to, will | insist on, be dead set on |
| feel | neutral | stubborn, often against advice |
| example | 他**要**去。 (He wants to go.) | 他**非要**去。 (He insists on going, no matter what.) |

## About to: 快要…了, 就要…了

| Word | Zhuyin | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **快要…了** | ㄎㄨㄞˋ ㄧㄠˋ…ㄌㄜ˙ | kuài yào… le | about to |
| **就要…了** | ㄐㄧㄡˋ ㄧㄠˋ…ㄌㄜ˙ | jiù yào… le | about to (can take a time word) |

> 快要下雨了。 → *Kuài yào xià yǔ le.* (It's about to rain.)

> 我們明天就要走了。 → *Wǒmen míngtiān jiù yào zǒu le.* (We're leaving tomorrow.)

**Trap:** with a specific time word, use **就要**, not 快要: 明天快要走了 ✗.

## Forbidding: 不要, 別

| Word | Zhuyin | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **不要** | ㄅㄨˊ ㄧㄠˋ | bú yào | don't! (command); don't want |
| **別** | ㄅㄧㄝˊ | bié | don't! (shorter, more spoken) |

> 不要跑！ → *Bú yào pǎo!* (Don't run!)

> 別擔心。 → *Bié dānxīn.* (Don't worry.)

## Negatives at a glance

The negative depends on which meaning of 要 you mean:

| Positive | Negative | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 要 (want) | **不想** ㄅㄨˋ ㄒㄧㄤˇ *bù xiǎng* | 我不想去。 (I don't want to go.) |
| 要 / 需要 / 得 (need) | **不用** | 你不用去。 (You don't need to go.) |
| 必須 (must) | **不必** | 你不必去。 (You needn't go.) |
| 要 + verb (command) | **不要 / 別** | 不要去！ (Don't go!) |

**Trap:** 我不要 means "I don't want (it)!" and can sound blunt or childish. For "I don't want to…," **不想** is softer and more natural.

## Look alike, but not "want" or "need"

| Word | Zhuyin | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **只要** | ㄓˇ ㄧㄠˋ | zhǐyào | as long as (conjunction) |
| **要是** | ㄧㄠˋ ㄕˋ | yàoshì | if (see [要是](./y.md)) |
| **要不 / 要不然** | ㄧㄠˋ ㄅㄨˋ / ㄧㄠˋ ㄅㄨˋ ㄖㄢˊ | yàobù / yàobùrán | otherwise, or else |
| **重要** | ㄓㄨㄥˋ ㄧㄠˋ | zhòngyào | important |

> 只要你努力，就會成功。 → *Zhǐyào nǐ nǔlì, jiù huì chénggōng.* (As long as you work hard, you'll succeed.)

> 快點，要不就來不及了。 → *Kuài diǎn, yàobù jiù láibùjí le.* (Hurry up, or we'll be late.)
