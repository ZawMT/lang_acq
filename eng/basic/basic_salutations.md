# Basic Salutations

An applied note on everyday greetings, farewells, and the polite phrases used to open and close conversations, letters, and emails.

## Greetings by time of day

| Time | Greeting |
|---|---|
| Morning | Good morning. |
| Afternoon | Good afternoon. |
| Evening / Night | Good evening. |

Note: there's no separate greeting for "night" — "**Good evening**" covers greeting someone in the evening or at night. "**Good night**" is not a greeting at all; see [Farewells](#farewells).

## Meeting someone

| Formal | Informal |
|---|---|
| Good morning, how are you? | Hi! How's it going? |
| It's a pleasure to meet you. | Nice to meet you! |
| How do you do? | Hey, what's up? |

Note: "**How do you do?**" is used only the first time you meet someone, and is fairly formal/old-fashioned (mostly British). It isn't really a question about wellbeing — the expected reply is often just "How do you do?" right back. This is different from "**How are you?**", which can be asked of anyone, anytime, including people you already know.

## Farewells

| Formal | Informal |
|---|---|
| Goodbye. | Bye! |
| Have a good day. | See you later! |
| It was a pleasure meeting you. | Take care! |
| Good night. | Night! |

Note: "**Good night**" is used only when parting — leaving someone's company in the evening, or right before bed.

## Addressing people (titles)

| Title | Used for |
|---|---|
| Mr. | any man |
| Mrs. | a married woman |
| Ms. | any woman (marital status unspecified) |
| Miss | an unmarried woman |
| Dr. | a person with a doctorate/medical degree |
| Sir / Madam | a person whose name you don't know |

- Sentence: Excuse me, **Sir**, could I ask a question?

## Written salutations (letters and emails)

| Formal | Informal |
|---|---|
| Dear Mr. Smith, | Hi John, |
| Dear Sir/Madam, | Hey there, |
| To Whom It May Concern, | Hello, |

## Written closings (letters and emails)

| Formal | Informal |
|---|---|
| Yours faithfully, *(when you don't know the name)* | Best, |
| Yours sincerely, *(when you know the name)* | Cheers, |
| Best regards, | Take care, |
