# Reported Speech

Reported (indirect) speech is how we retell what someone said, without quoting their exact words. It relies on the [tenses](./14_tenses.md) and [verb forms](./13_verb_forms.md) covered earlier, since reporting usually shifts the tense back one step.

## Direct vs. indirect speech

| Type | Definition | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Direct speech | the exact words, in quotation marks | She said, "**I am** tired." |
| Indirect (reported) speech | the meaning retold, no quotation marks, tense usually shifts back | She said (that) **she was** tired. |

## Tense backshift

When the reporting verb is in the past (said, told, asked), the tense in the quote usually moves one step into the past.

| Direct speech | Reported speech |
|---|---|
| Present simple: "I **work** here." | Past simple: She said she **worked** there. |
| Present continuous: "I **am working**." | Past continuous: She said she **was working**. |
| Present perfect: "I **have finished**." | Past perfect: She said she **had finished**. |
| Past simple: "I **finished**." | Past perfect: She said she **had finished**. |
| Past continuous: "I **was working**." | Past perfect continuous: She said she **had been working**. |
| will: "I **will** call." | would: She said she **would** call. |
| can: "I **can** swim." | could: She said she **could** swim. |
| may: "I **may** come." | might: She said she **might** come. |
| must: "I **must** go." | had to: She said she **had to** go. |

No backshift is needed when the fact is still true, general, or the reporting verb is in the present tense: "She says she **is** tired."

## Pronoun, time, and place changes

Since the reporter isn't the original speaker, pronouns and time/place references usually change too.

| Direct speech | Reported speech |
|---|---|
| I / we | he, she / they |
| my / our | his, her / their |
| this / these | that / those |
| now | then |
| today | that day |
| tomorrow | the next day |
| yesterday | the day before |
| here | there |

- Sentence: "**I** will finish **this today**." → He said **he** would finish **that** **the same day**.

## Reported questions

Reported questions drop the question word order and the question mark — they become a statement-shaped clause.

| Type | Direct speech | Reported speech |
|---|---|---|
| Yes/no question | "**Are** you tired?" | She asked **if/whether** I **was** tired. |
| Wh- question | "**Where do** you live?" | She asked **where** I **lived**. |

Note: no auxiliary "do/does/did" and no inversion — the word order goes back to normal subject + verb.

## Reported commands and requests

Commands and requests are reported using an infinitive ("to" + base form), not a full clause.

| Direct speech | Reported speech |
|---|---|
| "**Close** the door." | She told him **to close** the door. |
| "**Please help** me." | She asked him **to help** her. |
| "**Don't be** late." | She told him **not to be** late. |
