# Pronouns

A pronoun replaces a noun so we don't have to keep repeating it (see [Pronoun](./03_epos.md#pronoun)). English has several sub-types, each doing a different job.

## Personal pronouns

Stand in for specific people or things, and change form depending on whether they're the subject or object of the verb.

| Person | Subject | Object |
|---|---|---|
| 1st singular | I | me |
| 2nd singular | you | you |
| 3rd singular | he / she / it | him / her / it |
| 1st plural | we | us |
| 2nd plural | you | you |
| 3rd plural | they | them |

- Sentence: **She** called **him** yesterday.

## Possessive pronouns

Show ownership, and stand alone in place of a noun (compare with possessive determiners like "my", "your" — those come before a noun, e.g. "my book" — see [determiners](./03_epos.md#beyond-the-eight-parts)).

| Determiner (before a noun) | Possessive pronoun (stands alone) |
|---|---|
| my | mine |
| your | yours |
| his | his |
| her | hers |
| its | its |
| our | ours |
| their | theirs |

- Sentence: That book is **mine**, not **yours**.

## Reflexive pronouns

Refer back to the subject of the sentence, showing the action affects the same person/thing that did it.

| Pronoun |
|---|
| myself |
| yourself |
| himself / herself / itself |
| ourselves |
| yourselves |
| themselves |

- Sentence: She taught **herself** to swim.

## Demonstrative pronouns

Point to specific things, based on distance and number.

| | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| Near | this | these |
| Far | that | those |

| Pronoun | Example sentence |
|---|---|
| this | **This** is my favorite. |
| that | **That** looks expensive. |
| these | **These** are fresh. |
| those | **Those** are yours. |

## Interrogative pronouns

Used to ask questions.

| Pronoun | Asks about | Example sentence |
|---|---|---|
| who | a person (subject) | **Who** called? |
| whom | a person (object) | **Whom** did you call? |
| whose | ownership | **Whose** is this bag? |
| which | a choice among options | **Which** do you prefer? |
| what | a thing or idea | **What** did they want? |

## Relative pronouns

Introduce a relative clause, connecting it back to a noun mentioned earlier.

| Pronoun | Refers to | Example sentence |
|---|---|---|
| who | people (subject) | The woman **who** called is my aunt. |
| whom | people (object) | The man **whom** you met is my uncle. |
| whose | ownership | The boy **whose** dog barked is my neighbor. |
| which | things | The book **which** I borrowed is overdue. |
| that | people or things | The car **that** I bought is red. |

## Indefinite pronouns

Refer to people or things without saying exactly who or what.

| Group | Examples |
|---|---|
| People | someone, anyone, everyone, no one, somebody, nobody |
| Things | something, anything, everything, nothing |
| Quantity | all, some, many, few, several, none, one, each |

- Sentence: **Everyone** brought **something**, but **no one** brought a cake.

## Reciprocal pronouns

Show a mutual action or relationship between two or more people.

| Pronoun | Used for |
|---|---|
| each other | two people/things |
| one another | more than two (traditionally, though often used interchangeably today) |

- Sentence: They looked at **each other** and smiled.
