# Tenses

A tense is how we combine [verb forms](./13_verb_forms.md) with auxiliary verbs to place an action in time. English tenses are organized along two independent axes:

- **Time** — past, present, or future
- **Aspect** — simple, continuous (progressive), perfect, or perfect continuous

3 times × 4 aspects = **12 tenses**.

## The 12-tense grid

| | Simple | Continuous | Perfect | Perfect continuous |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Present** | I **walk** | I **am walking** | I **have walked** | I **have been walking** |
| **Past** | I **walked** | I **was walking** | I **had walked** | I **had been walking** |
| **Future** | I **will walk** | I **will be walking** | I **will have walked** | I **will have been walking** |

## Present tenses

| Tense | Built from | Example sentence |
|---|---|---|
| Present simple | base form (+ "-s" for he/she/it) | She **walks** to work. |
| Present continuous | am/is/are + present participle | She **is walking** to work now. |
| Present perfect | has/have + past participle | She **has walked** 5 miles today. |
| Present perfect continuous | has/have been + present participle | She **has been walking** for an hour. |

## Past tenses

| Tense | Built from | Example sentence |
|---|---|---|
| Past simple | past simple form | She **walked** to work yesterday. |
| Past continuous | was/were + present participle | She **was walking** when it started to rain. |
| Past perfect | had + past participle | She **had walked** 5 miles before lunch. |
| Past perfect continuous | had been + present participle | She **had been walking** for an hour before she stopped. |

## Future tenses

| Tense | Built from | Example sentence |
|---|---|---|
| Future simple | will + base form | She **will walk** to work tomorrow. |
| Future continuous | will be + present participle | She **will be walking** at 8 AM. |
| Future perfect | will have + past participle | She **will have walked** 5 miles by noon. |
| Future perfect continuous | will have been + present participle | By then, she **will have been walking** for an hour. |
