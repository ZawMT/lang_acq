# Prepositions and Conjunctions

Prepositions and conjunctions are both **function words** — a small, fixed ("closed class") set of words whose job is to connect and relate other words, rather than carry meaning on their own (compare with **content words** like nouns and verbs, an "open class" that keeps growing). See [Preposition](./03_epos.md#preposition) and [Conjunction](./03_epos.md#conjunction) for their basic definitions.

## Prepositions

A preposition shows how a noun (or pronoun) relates to the rest of the sentence — usually place, time, direction, or another relationship.

### Place

| Preposition | Example sentence |
|---|---|
| in | The keys are **in** the drawer. |
| on | The book is **on** the table. |
| at | She's waiting **at** the door. |
| under | The cat is **under** the bed. |
| above | The plane flew **above** the clouds. |
| between | The park is **between** two streets. |
| among | She was standing **among** the crowd. |
| behind | The car is parked **behind** the house. |
| next to | He sat **next to** me. |

### Time

| Preposition | Example sentence |
|---|---|
| at | We'll meet **at** 5 PM. |
| on | The party is **on** Friday. |
| in | She was born **in** 1990. |
| before | Call me **before** noon. |
| after | We left **after** dinner. |
| during | It rained **during** the trip. |
| since | I've been here **since** morning. |
| until | She'll stay **until** Sunday. |

### Direction / movement

| Preposition | Example sentence |
|---|---|
| to | She walked **to** the shop. |
| into | He jumped **into** the pool. |
| onto | The cat climbed **onto** the roof. |
| through | We drove **through** the tunnel. |
| across | They swam **across** the river. |
| along | We walked **along** the beach. |
| towards | The dog ran **towards** us. |

### Other relationships

| Preposition | Relationship | Example sentence |
|---|---|---|
| by | agent | The book was written **by** her. |
| with | instrument / accompaniment | I ate **with** a fork. She came **with** her sister. |
| like | comparison | He sings **like** a professional. |
| about | topic | We talked **about** the weather. |

## Conjunctions

A conjunction joins words, phrases, or clauses together.

### Coordinating conjunctions

Join two equal, independent parts. Remembered with the acronym **FANBOYS**.

| Conjunction | Example sentence |
|---|---|
| for | She left early, **for** she was tired. |
| and | I like tea **and** coffee. |
| nor | He doesn't smoke, **nor** does he drink. |
| but | It's small **but** comfortable. |
| or | Do you want tea **or** coffee? |
| yet | It's expensive, **yet** worth it. |
| so | It was late, **so** we left. |

### Subordinating conjunctions

Join a dependent clause to an independent clause (see [Clause](./04_sentence_structure.md#clause)).

| Conjunction | Example sentence |
|---|---|
| because | She left early **because** she was tired. |
| although | **Although** it rained, we went out. |
| if | I'll come **if** I have time. |
| when | Call me **when** you arrive. |
| since | **Since** you asked, I'll explain. |
| unless | We won't go **unless** it stops raining. |
| while | She read **while** he cooked. |
| whereas | He's tall, **whereas** she's short. |

### Correlative conjunctions

Work in pairs to join equal parts.

| Conjunction pair | Example sentence |
|---|---|
| either...or | **Either** you go **or** you stay. |
| neither...nor | **Neither** the cat **nor** the dog is here. |
| both...and | **Both** he **and** she agreed. |
| not only...but also | She is **not only** smart **but also** kind. |
| whether...or | I don't know **whether** to laugh **or** cry. |
