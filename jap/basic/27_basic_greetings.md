# Basic Greetings and Everyday Phrases (挨拶)

Japanese greetings (挨拶（あいさつ）) are **set phrases** tied to specific situations: the time of day, leaving and coming home, eating, starting and finishing work. Many change with **politeness** (see [Politeness and Keigo](./08_politeness_keigo.md)), and most come with a **bow** (お辞儀（じぎ）): a slight nod for casual moments, a deeper bow for formal ones.

## Hello

| Phrase | Level | When |
|---|---|---|
| **おはようございます** | polite | morning; also the first greeting at work, any time of day |
| **おはよう** | casual | morning, to friends and family |
| **こんにちは** | neutral | daytime (late morning to early evening) |
| **こんばんは** | neutral | evening |
| **どうも** | casual-neutral | quick hello (also "thanks") |

**Trap:** **こんにちは** is for people **outside** your circle. You don't say it to family, and colleagues use おはようございます or お疲（つか）れ様（さま）です instead.

**こんにちは** and **こんばんは** end in **は** (said **wa**), not ✗ こんにちわ.

## Goodbye

| Phrase | Level | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **さようなら** | neutral | a longer parting; teachers and students at school. Rare between colleagues or friends |
| **失礼（しつれい）します** | polite | leaving a room, an office, a call |
| **お先（さき）に失礼します** | polite | leaving work before others |
| **お疲れ様でした** | polite | to someone leaving work, after finishing something together |
| **では、また** / **じゃあ、また** | neutral / casual | see you |
| **またね** / **じゃあね** / **バイバイ** | casual | bye |
| **また明日（あした）** | casual-neutral | see you tomorrow |
| **気（き）をつけて** | casual-neutral | take care (on the way) |

At work, when someone leaves before you:

- A: お先に失礼します。 (I'm leaving first.)
- B: お疲れ様でした。 (Thanks for your hard work.)

**Trap:** **ご苦労様（くろうさま）です** also means "thank you for your work", but it's said **from a superior to a subordinate**. To superiors, always use **お疲れ様です**.

## Leaving home and coming back

Japanese has fixed pairs for leaving and returning, used at home and at work:

| Situation | The one leaving / arriving | The one staying |
|---|---|---|
| leaving | **行（い）ってきます** (I'll go and come back) | **行ってらっしゃい** (Go and come back) |
| returning | **ただいま** (I'm home) | **おかえりなさい** / **おかえり** (Welcome back) |

Polite: **行ってまいります** (humble), **行ってらっしゃいませ**.

## Thank you

| Phrase | Level |
|---|---|
| **ありがとうございます** | polite |
| **ありがとうございました** | polite, for something finished |
| **どうもありがとうございます** | very polite |
| **ありがとう** | casual |
| **どうも** | casual, quick |
| **すみません** | polite thanks when someone took trouble for you |
| **恐（おそ）れ入（い）ります** | very polite (business) |

**Replies:**

| Phrase | Feel |
|---|---|
| **いいえ** / **いえいえ** | not at all (most common) |
| **どういたしまして** | you're welcome (correct, but can sound a little stiff) |
| **こちらこそ** | thank *you* (the pleasure is mine) |

## Sorry and excuse me

| Phrase | Level | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **すみません** | polite | sorry; excuse me; thanks (all-purpose) |
| **ごめんなさい** | neutral | sorry (personal apology; to friends, family, children) |
| **ごめん** / **ごめんね** | casual | sorry |
| **申（もう）し訳（わけ）ありません** | very polite | sincere apology (business, serious mistakes) |
| **申し訳ございません** | most polite | formal apology (service, business) |
| **失礼しました** | polite | excuse me (small mistakes) |

**すみません** is one of the most useful words: getting a waiter's attention (すみません！), squeezing past someone, apologizing, or thanking someone who went to trouble for you.

## Meeting people

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **はじめまして** | Nice to meet you (first words on meeting) |
| **〜です / 〜と申（もう）します** | I'm ~ / My name is ~ (humble) |
| **よろしくお願（ねが）いします** | Nice to meet you; please treat me well |
| **どうぞよろしくお願いいたします** | (very polite) |
| **こちらこそ、よろしくお願いします** | Likewise, nice to meet you |

A typical self-introduction (自己紹介（じこしょうかい）):

- はじめまして。マイクと申します。アメリカから来ました。どうぞよろしくお願いします。 (Nice to meet you. My name is Mike. I'm from the U.S. I look forward to working with you.)

### How are you?

| Phrase | Use |
|---|---|
| **お元気（げんき）ですか** | How have you been? (after not meeting for a while, not daily) |
| **元気？** | How are you? (casual) |
| **お久（ひさ）しぶりです** / **久しぶり** | Long time no see |
| **おかげさまで、元気です** | I'm well, thank you (literally "thanks to you") |

**Trap:** **お元気ですか** is not a daily "how are you". It's for people you haven't seen in a while, and in letters.

## Meals

| Phrase | When |
|---|---|
| **いただきます** | before eating (thanks for the food) |
| **ごちそうさまでした** | after eating; also to thank someone who paid |
| **おいしい！** | It's good! |

## Visiting

| Phrase | Who says it |
|---|---|
| **お邪魔（じゃま）します** | guest, entering someone's home |
| **どうぞ、お上（あ）がりください** | host: please come in |
| **お邪魔しました** | guest, leaving |
| **失礼します** | entering an office or a teacher's room |
| **つまらないものですが…** | guest giving a gift ("it's a small thing, but…"; now often replaced by ほんの気持（きも）ちですが) |

## Shops and restaurants

| Phrase | Who | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **いらっしゃいませ** | staff | Welcome (you don't need to reply) |
| **何名様（なんめいさま）ですか** | staff | How many people? |
| **〜をください** / **〜をお願いします** | customer | ~, please |
| **これ、いくらですか** | customer | How much is this? |
| **お会計（かいけい）をお願いします** | customer | The bill, please |
| **少々（しょうしょう）お待ちください** | staff | Please wait a moment |
| **ありがとうございました** | staff | Thank you (as you leave) |

## Health and wishes

| Phrase | Use |
|---|---|
| **お大事（だいじ）に** | Get well soon (to someone sick) |
| **気をつけて** | Take care |
| **頑張（がんば）って(ください)** | Good luck, do your best |
| **おめでとうございます** | Congratulations |
| **お誕生日（たんじょうび）おめでとう** | Happy birthday |
| **おやすみなさい** / **おやすみ** | Good night (before bed) |

## Seasonal greetings

| Phrase | When |
|---|---|
| **良（よ）いお年（とし）を** | before New Year (last meeting of the year): Have a good New Year |
| **あけましておめでとうございます** | after New Year: Happy New Year |
| **今年（ことし）もよろしくお願いします** | New Year: I look forward to this year with you too |

## On the phone

| Phrase | Use |
|---|---|
| **もしもし** | Hello (personal calls; not used when answering business calls) |
| **はい、〇〇です** | answering: Yes, this is ~ |
| **〇〇さんはいらっしゃいますか** | Is ~ there? |
| **少々お待ちください** | Please hold |
| **失礼します** | before hanging up |
