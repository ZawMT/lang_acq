# Pronouns (代名詞)

Japanese has many personal pronouns, but uses them **much less** than you might expect. The subject is often left out (see [Sentence Structure](./05_sentence_structure.md#leaving-things-out)), and people are usually referred to by **name or title**.

## First person: I

The choice of "I" shows gender, age, and how formal you're being.

| Word | Who uses it | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| **わたくし** (私) | anyone | very formal: speeches, business |
| **わたし** (私) | anyone (default) | neutral, polite; casual for women |
| **僕（ぼく）** | men, boys | soft, casual to neutral |
| **俺（おれ）** | men | casual, rough, confident; not for formal settings |
| **あたし** | women | casual, cute |
| **うち** | women, especially in western Japan | casual |
| **自分（じぶん）** | sometimes men in sports, military | stiff; also means "oneself" |

- Use **わたし** in polite speech. At work, men also use **わたし** or **わたくし**, and **僕** in less formal moments.
- Children often call themselves by their **own name**: ゆきも行く！ (Yuki's going too!)

## Second person: you

**Avoid "you" where you can.** Using a pronoun for the listener can sound distant or rude. Use their **name + さん**, a **title**, or nothing at all:

- 田中（たなか）さんは行きますか。 (Are you going, Mr. Tanaka?)
- 先生（せんせい）、これは何ですか。 (Teacher, what is this?)
- 行きますか。 (Are you going?)

| Word | Feel |
|---|---|
| **あなた** | neutral in writing (forms, ads, songs); in speech it can sound distant or condescending. Wives sometimes call husbands あなた. |
| **君（きみ）** | from a man to a junior or partner; also in songs |
| **あんた** | casual, can sound rude |
| **お前（おまえ）** | rough; between close male friends, or insulting |
| **貴様（きさま）** | very insulting |

**Trap:** never call a boss, teacher, or customer **あなた**. Use their title or name.

## Third person: he, she, they

| Word | Meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **彼（かれ）** | he | also "boyfriend" |
| **彼女（かのじょ）** | she | also "girlfriend" |
| **あの人（ひと）** | that person | common in speech |
| **あの方（かた）** | that person | polite |
| **あいつ / こいつ / そいつ** | that guy | casual, rude, or very friendly |

In conversation, people usually repeat the **name**: 田中さんは？ — 田中さんはもう帰りました。 (What about Tanaka? — Tanaka has gone home already.)

## Plurals

Add a suffix to make a group:

| Suffix | Feel | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **〜たち** | neutral | 私たち (we), 子供（こども）たち (children), 田中さんたち (Tanaka and the others) |
| **〜ら** | casual, sometimes rough | 僕ら, 彼ら (they), あいつら |
| **〜方（がた）** | respectful | あなた方, 先生方 (the teachers) |
| **〜ども** | humble (for your own group) | 私ども (we, the company) |

**Trap:** 田中さん**たち** means "Tanaka and the people with him", not "several Tanakas".

## Reflexive: 自分

**自分（じぶん）** means "oneself", referring back to the subject:

- 自分で作りました。 (I made it myself.)
- 自分の部屋（へや） (one's own room)

**自分で** = by oneself, on one's own.

## こ・そ・あ・ど words

Japanese builds "this / that / which" words in sets of four, from **こ, そ, あ, ど**:

| | こ (near me) | そ (near you) | あ (far from both) | ど (which?) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| thing | **これ** | **それ** | **あれ** | **どれ** |
| + noun | **この** 本（ほん） | **その** 本 | **あの** 本 | **どの** 本 |
| place | **ここ** | **そこ** | **あそこ** | **どこ** |
| direction (polite) | **こちら** | **そちら** | **あちら** | **どちら** |
| direction (casual) | **こっち** | **そっち** | **あっち** | **どっち** |
| kind | **こんな** | **そんな** | **あんな** | **どんな** |
| manner | **こう** | **そう** | **ああ** | **どう** |

- **これ**は何ですか。 (What's this?) — **それ**はペンです。 (That's a pen.)
- **この**かばんは**いくら**ですか。 (How much is this bag?)
- トイレは**どこ**ですか。 (Where's the toilet?) — **あそこ**です。 (Over there.)

**Trap:** これ/それ/あれ stand alone; この/その/あの must come **before a noun**. ✗ この は本です → **これ**は本です / **この**本は…

**こちら** etc. are also polite words for people and places: **こちら**は田中さんです。 (This is Mr. Tanaka.) / **どちら**からですか。 (Where are you from?)

### そ vs あ in conversation

For things that aren't physically present:

- **あ** = something **both** speaker and listener know: **あの**店、おいしかったね。 (That restaurant was good, wasn't it?)
- **そ** = something only **one** of you knows, or that was just mentioned: A: 駅前（えきまえ）に新しい店ができたよ。B: **その**店、高い？ (A: A new shop opened by the station. B: Is that shop expensive?)

## Question words

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **何（なに / なん）** | what |
| **誰（だれ）** / **どなた** (polite) | who |
| **いつ** | when |
| **どこ** / **どちら** (polite) | where |
| **どれ** / **どっち** / **どちら** | which (of 3+ / of 2) |
| **どう** / **いかが** (polite) | how |
| **なぜ** / **どうして** / **なんで** (casual) | why |
| **いくら** | how much (price) |
| **いくつ** | how many; how old |

**何** is read **なん** before d, n, t sounds and counters: **なん**ですか, **なん**時, **なん**人; otherwise **なに**: **なに**を, **なに**が.

### Question word + か / も / でも

| Pattern | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| + **か** | some- | 誰か (someone), 何か (something), どこか (somewhere), いつか (someday) |
| + **も** + negative | no- | 誰も来ない (no one comes), 何も食べない (eat nothing), どこにも行かない (go nowhere) |
| + **でも** | any- (every) | 誰でも (anyone), 何でも (anything), いつでも (anytime) |
| いつも / どこも | always / everywhere | いつも忙（いそが）しい (always busy) |

- 何**か**飲みますか。 (Will you drink something?)
- 何**も**いりません。 (I don't need anything.)
- 何**でも**いいです。 (Anything's fine.)
