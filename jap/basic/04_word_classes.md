# Word Classes (品詞)

Japanese school grammar divides words into **10 word classes (品詞, ひんし)**. Two questions sort them:

1. Can it stand on its own as a word (**自立語（じりつご）**), or does it only attach to other words (**付属語（ふぞくご）**)?
2. Does it **conjugate** (**活用（かつよう）** = change its ending)?

| Class | Japanese term | Stands alone? | Conjugates? | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Noun (incl. pronouns) | 名詞（めいし） | yes | no | 本, 学生, 私, 東京 |
| Verb | 動詞（どうし） | yes | **yes** | 食べる, 行く, する |
| い-adjective | 形容詞（けいようし） | yes | **yes** | 高い, 新しい, いい |
| な-adjective | 形容動詞（けいようどうし） | yes | **yes** | 静か(だ), きれい(だ) |
| Adverb | 副詞（ふくし） | yes | no | とても, もう, ゆっくり |
| Pre-noun adjectival | 連体詞（れんたいし） | yes | no | この, あの, 大きな |
| Conjunction | 接続詞（せつぞくし） | yes | no | しかし, だから, そして |
| Interjection | 感動詞（かんどうし） | yes | no | ああ, はい, もしもし |
| Auxiliary verb | 助動詞（じょどうし） | no | **yes** | ます, ない, た, です, られる |
| Particle | 助詞（じょし） | no | no | は, が, を, に, よ |

Textbooks for learners usually call 形容詞 **い-adjectives** and 形容動詞 **な-adjectives** (see [Adjectives](./12_adjectives.md)). These notes use those names.

## The conjugating words: 用言

**Verbs, い-adjectives, and な-adjectives** together are called **用言（ようげん）**. They can be the **predicate** (the word that ends a sentence) by themselves, and they change form for tense, negation, and politeness:

- 食べ**る** → 食べ**ない** → 食べ**た** → 食べ**ます** (eat)
- 高**い** → 高**くない** → 高**かった** → 高**いです** (expensive)
- 静か**だ** → 静か**じゃない** → 静か**だった** → 静か**です** (quiet)

Nouns are called **体言（たいげん）**. They don't conjugate; they need **だ / です** to be a predicate: 学生**だ**, 学生**です** (is a student).

## 名詞: nouns

Nouns name things, people, places, and ideas. They take **particles** to show their role (see [Particles](./06_particles.md)). Japanese nouns have no plural ending, no gender, and no articles.

- 本 (book, books), 猫 (cat, cats), 水 (water)

**Pronouns** (代名詞, だいめいし) are a type of noun in Japanese school grammar (see [Pronouns](./09_pronouns.md)). So are **numbers**, but they're used with counters (see [Numbers and Counters](./11_numbers_counters.md)).

## 動詞: verbs

Verbs show actions, events, and existence. The **dictionary form** always ends in an **-u** sound: 書**く**, 飲**む**, 食べ**る**, あ**る**. See [Verb Groups and Conjugation](./07_verb_conjugation.md).

## 形容詞 and 形容動詞: adjectives

Both describe qualities and states, and both can modify a noun or end a sentence:

| | い-adjective | な-adjective |
|---|---|---|
| Before a noun | 高**い**山 (a tall mountain) | 静か**な**町 (a quiet town) |
| As predicate | 山が高**い**. | 町が静か**だ**. |

## 副詞: adverbs

Adverbs modify verbs, adjectives, or the whole sentence. They don't conjugate: **とても**高い (very expensive), **ゆっくり**話す (speak slowly). See [Adverbs](./13_adverbs.md).

## 連体詞: pre-noun words

**連体詞** only come **directly before a noun** and never change form. There aren't many:

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| この / その / あの / どの | this / that / that over there / which |
| 大きな / 小さな | big / small (only before nouns) |
| ある | a certain: **ある**日 (one day) |
| いわゆる | so-called |
| たいした | great, considerable (usually with negative) |

**Trap:** **ある** as 連体詞 (a certain) is a different word from the verb ある (to exist): **ある**人が来た (A certain person came).

## 接続詞: conjunctions

Conjunctions link sentences or clauses: そして (and then), でも / しかし (but), だから (so), それに (besides). See [Connecting Clauses](./20_connecting_clauses.md#conjunctions-接続詞).

## 感動詞: interjections

Words that stand alone to express feeling, answer, or call: **ああ**, **はい**, **いいえ**, **ねえ**, **もしもし**. See [Sentence-final Particles and Interjections](./24_sentence_final_particles.md).

## 助動詞: auxiliary verbs

**助動詞** attach to 用言 and add meaning such as negation, past, politeness, passive, or wanting. They conjugate themselves, and they stack in a fixed order:

| Auxiliary | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| ます | polite | 行き**ます** |
| ない / ぬ | negative | 行か**ない** |
| た | past, completed | 行っ**た** |
| たい | want to | 行き**たい** |
| (ら)れる | passive, potential, respect | 書か**れる** |
| (さ)せる | causative | 書か**せる** |
| だ / です | copula (is) | 学生**だ**, 学生**です** |
| らしい, ようだ, そうだ | seems, hear that | 雨**らしい** |

Stacking: 書か**せ**・**られ**・**たく**・**なかっ**・**た** (didn't want to be made to write).

## 助詞: particles

Particles are short words that **attach after** another word, with no change in form. They mark a word's role (が, を, に), link words (と, や), or add the speaker's attitude at the end of a sentence (よ, ね). See [Particles](./06_particles.md) and [Sentence-final Particles](./24_sentence_final_particles.md).
