# Idioms and Fixed Expressions (慣用句・ことわざ・四字熟語)

Japanese has three main kinds of fixed expressions:

| Kind | Japanese | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Idioms** | 慣用句（かんようく） | phrases whose meaning isn't the sum of the words, often using body parts |
| **Proverbs** | ことわざ | short sayings with a lesson |
| **Four-character compounds** | 四字熟語（よじじゅくご） | four kanji expressing an idea, often from Chinese classics |

## Body-part idioms (慣用句)

Many everyday idioms use body parts.

### 手 (hand)

| Idiom | Literally | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **手を貸（か）す** | lend a hand | help |
| **手が足（た）りない** | hands aren't enough | be short-handed |
| **手が出（で）ない** | hand doesn't come out | can't afford; can't manage |
| **手を焼（や）く** | burn one's hand | have trouble with, can't handle |
| **手に入（い）れる** | put in hand | get, obtain |
| **手が離（はな）せない** | can't let go | be tied up, busy |
| **手を抜（ぬ）く** | pull out the hand | cut corners |
| **猫（ねこ）の手も借（か）りたい** | want to borrow even a cat's paws | be extremely busy |

### 顔 (face) and 口 (mouth)

| Idiom | Literally | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **顔（かお）が広（ひろ）い** | face is wide | know a lot of people |
| **顔を出（だ）す** | show one's face | drop by, show up |
| **口（くち）が堅（かた）い** | mouth is hard | can keep a secret |
| **口が軽（かる）い** | mouth is light | can't keep a secret |
| **口に合（あ）う** | fits the mouth | suit one's taste |

### 目 (eye), 耳 (ear), 鼻 (nose)

| Idiom | Literally | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **目（め）がない** | have no eyes | love, can't resist: 甘（あま）いものに目がない (I can't resist sweets) |
| **目を通（とお）す** | pass the eyes through | look over, skim |
| **耳（みみ）が痛（いた）い** | ears hurt | hit a nerve (criticism that's true) |
| **耳にする** | do with the ear | happen to hear |
| **鼻（はな）が高（たか）い** | nose is high | be proud |

### 頭 (head), 腹 (belly), 足 (foot), 首 (neck)

| Idiom | Literally | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **頭（あたま）が固（かた）い** | head is hard | stubborn, inflexible |
| **頭に来（く）る** | comes to the head | get angry |
| **腹（はら）が立（た）つ** | belly stands up | get angry |
| **足（あし）を洗（あら）う** | wash one's feet | quit (a bad life), go straight |
| **足が出る** | feet stick out | go over budget |
| **首（くび）になる** | become a neck | get fired |
| **首を長（なが）くして待（ま）つ** | wait with a long neck | wait eagerly |

### 気 (spirit, mind)

**気（き）** appears in dozens of essential expressions:

| Expression | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **気をつける** | be careful |
| **気になる** | be curious about, bother one |
| **気にする** | worry about, mind |
| **気に入（い）る** | like, be pleased with |
| **気がする** | have a feeling that |
| **気が合（あ）う** | get along |
| **気が利（き）く** | be thoughtful, attentive |
| **気が短（みじか）い** | be short-tempered |
| **気分（きぶん）がいい / 悪い** | feel good / unwell |

- 気を**つけて**ね。 (Take care.)
- このかばん、気に**入り**ました。 (I like this bag.)
- 雨が降（ふ）る気が**する**。 (I have a feeling it'll rain.)

## Proverbs (ことわざ)

| Proverb | Literally | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **猿（さる）も木（き）から落（お）ちる** | even monkeys fall from trees | everyone makes mistakes |
| **七転（ななころ）び八起（やお）き** | fall seven times, get up eight | never give up |
| **花（はな）より団子（だんご）** | dumplings over flowers | practical things over beauty |
| **石（いし）の上にも三年（さんねん）** | three years even on a stone | perseverance pays off |
| **急（いそ）がば回（まわ）れ** | if in a hurry, go around | more haste, less speed |
| **塵（ちり）も積（つ）もれば山（やま）となる** | even dust piles up into a mountain | small things add up |
| **出（で）る杭（くい）は打（う）たれる** | the nail that sticks out gets hammered | standing out invites criticism |
| **雨降（あめふ）って地（じ）固（かた）まる** | after rain the ground hardens | things get better after trouble |
| **案（あん）ずるより産（う）むが易（やす）し** | giving birth is easier than worrying about it | it's easier than you fear |
| **井（い）の中（なか）の蛙（かわず）** | a frog in a well | someone with a narrow view |
| **猫（ねこ）に小判（こばん）** | gold coins to a cat | wasted on someone who can't appreciate it |
| **知（し）らぬが仏（ほとけ）** | not knowing is Buddha | ignorance is bliss |

## Four-character compounds (四字熟語)

| Compound | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **一期一会** | いちごいちえ | each meeting happens once; treasure it |
| **一生懸命** | いっしょうけんめい | with all one's might |
| **一石二鳥** | いっせきにちょう | two birds with one stone |
| **十人十色** | じゅうにんといろ | ten people, ten colors: everyone is different |
| **以心伝心** | いしんでんしん | understanding without words |
| **自業自得** | じごうじとく | you reap what you sow |
| **自画自賛** | じがじさん | praising oneself |
| **温故知新** | おんこちしん | learn the new by studying the old |
| **三日坊主** | みっかぼうず | a three-day monk: someone who gives up quickly |
| **一長一短** | いっちょういったん | having both merits and faults |

- 毎日**一生懸命**働（はたら）いている。 (I work as hard as I can every day.)
- ダイエットはいつも**三日坊主**だ。 (My diets never last.)

## Everyday fixed expressions

These are said constantly and have no exact translation. They're tied to situations:

| Expression | Use |
|---|---|
| **よろしくお願（ねが）いします** | Nice to meet you; please treat me well; thanks in advance (for a favor) |
| **お疲（つか）れ様（さま）です** | greeting colleagues; thanks for your hard work |
| **お世話（せわ）になっております** | (business) thank you for your continued support |
| **いただきます** | before eating |
| **ごちそうさまでした** | after eating |
| **仕方（しかた）がない / しょうがない** | it can't be helped |
| **頑張（がんば）って / 頑張ります** | do your best / I'll do my best |
| **お大事（だいじ）に** | get well soon |
| **お邪魔（じゃま）します** | (entering someone's home) sorry to intrude |
| **失礼（しつれい）します** | excuse me (entering, leaving, hanging up) |
| **もったいない** | what a waste |
| **せっかく** | since (we went to the trouble): せっかくだから行こう (Since we're here, let's go.) |
| **おかげさまで** | thanks to you (I'm well; it went well) |
